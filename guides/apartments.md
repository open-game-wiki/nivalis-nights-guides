## 1. District Housing & Real Estate Portfolio

In Nivalis, your apartment is more than just a place to sleep—it serves as your operational headquarters, a personal sanctuary from neon rain, and a vital **safe bed anchor** to avoid curfew collapse penalties.

As your net worth grows, you can acquire residences across three distinct city tiers:

| Residence Tier | District Location | Acquisition Cost | Daily Maintenance | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starter Canal Loft** | Lower Waterways Sector | Included in Prologue | 25 Credits / day | Basic bed, old radio, single hotplate stove |
| **Midtown High-Rise** | Central Commercial Plaza | 22,000 Credits | 75 Credits / day | Balcony overlooking flying car traffic, freight elevator |
| **Cloud-Level Penthouse** | Upper Stratosphere Tier | 95,000 Credits | 250 Credits / day | Panoramic skyline glass walls, private rooftop landing pad |

## 2. 620+ Furniture Catalog & Interior Design

Every apartment features a fully customizable grid-based interior design mode:

* **Comfort Essentials**: High-thread ergonomic futons, smart memory foam mattresses (speed up stamina recovery after long shifts).
* **Atmospheric Lighting**: Cyber-neon tubes, dimmable LED paper lanterns, ambient holo-projectors that play synth-wave visualizers.
* **Storage Systems**: Lockers and high-capacity pantries to stockpile rare cooking ingredients and aged liquor away from damp kitchen storerooms.
* **Leisure Corner**: Bookshelves, retro console emulators, record players, and display pedestals for tournament chess trophies.

## 3. Freelance Interior Decorator Contracts

Once you purchase the **Interior Design License** from City Hall (1,200 Credits), wealthy citizens will contract you to renovate their private residences:

1. **Aesthetic Briefs**: Clients specify requirements such as *"Industrial Brutalist"* or *"Neo-Tokyo Minimalist"*.
2. **Budget Management**: You are allocated a fixed corporate stipend (typically 5,000 to 12,000 Credits) to source matching furniture.
3. **Rating & Bonuses**: Fulfilling all color palette and functional requirements earns a 5-star review, awarding up to **3,500 Credits in profit margin bonuses**.

---

## 4. Bed Anchors & Curfew Respawn Mechanics

* Sleeping in your apartment bed completely restores your character's fatigue meter and saves daily world state.
* If you own multiple apartments across different sectors, each one acts as an active **Curfew Refuge Point**. When the 02:00 siren sounds, you can duck into whichever apartment is geographically closest rather than risking a long boat journey home.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Ava move into my apartment?
Yes! After completing Ava's romantic confession storyline and purchasing either the Midtown High-Rise or Cloud-Level Penthouse, you can invite Ava to move in. She will bring her custom tool rack and share morning coffee dialogues.

### Do unpaid apartment rents result in eviction?
If your bank account goes negative and fails to pay daily maintenance fees for 3 consecutive days, the landlord locks your elevator access until back rent plus late fees are settled.

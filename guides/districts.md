## Nivalis Urban Geography & Exploration Overview

Rising from the drowned sea-level canals to the sun-pierced clouds, the vertical cyberpunk metropolis of **Nivalis** is segmented into distinct geographic sectors. Rather than an abstract open world, each district functions as an autonomous urban biome with its own storefront capacity, local storyline quests, hidden supply crates, and specialized merchants.

---

## In-Game Map System & HUD Metrics

When inspecting the City Map HUD, four operational metrics monitor your district presence:

1. **Storefronts (Commercial Real Estate)**: Total leaseable business venues in the district, ranging from walk-up noodle counters to sprawling multi-tier diners.
2. **Active Quests**: Local narrative objectives and commercial contracts (such as the early-game *Noodle Bar Introduction* in Meridian Market).
3. **Collectibles & Crates**: Shows hidden supply crates discovered versus total available in the district (e.g., finding all 11 crates in Metro Hub).
4. **Day / Night & Curfew Status**: Monitors the operational time window before the city's 02:00 AM martial curfew takes effect.

### 1. Central Canyon (Commercial Real Estate Giant)
- **Storefront Capacity**: 10
- **Atmosphere**: A massive vertical chasm slicing through the skyline. Establishments cling to sheer cliff-like towers on both sides, connected by dizzying suspension bridges.
- **Business Strategy**: Ideal for full-service dinners and synth-steak grills commanding high ticket prices.

### 2. Metro Hub (Transit & High Foot Traffic)
- **Storefront Capacity**: 9
- **Atmosphere**: The transit nexus connecting lower canals with upper residential towers. Constant commuter trains bring steady daytime foot traffic.
- **Business Strategy**: Best suited for rapid walk-up counters, grab-and-go bao buns, and hot VegCoffee stands.

### 3. Hive Mall (Enclosed Megamall & Crates)
- **Storefront Capacity**: 7
- **Atmosphere**: A multi-tiered retail hub completely protected from Nivalis's perpetual acid rain.
- **Exploration Value**: Features **13 hidden supply crates** containing rare crafting components, furniture blueprints, and seed packets.

### 4. Meridian Market (Your Commercial Launchpad)
- **Storefront Capacity**: 6
- **Atmosphere**: One of the oldest canal-level markets in Nivalis.
- **Significance**: Home to the opening quest *Noodle Bar Introduction*, Thaddeus's Ramen Noir stall, and primary wholesale spice/noodle merchants.

## Coastal, Industrial & Outer Districts

Beyond the core four commercial sectors, specialized zones offer essential resources:

- **Seaside Boardwalk**: Waterfront docks where you meet Ava, dock your motorboat, and fish along canal reefs.
- **Industrial District**: Houses rentable hydroponic greenhouses for growing your own scallions, mushrooms, and wasabi.
- **Calypso Island**: High-stakes entertainment sanctuary hosting the prestigious City Chess Qualifiers and VIP casinos.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which district has the most leaseable business storefronts?
Central Canyon has the highest commercial capacity with 10 confirmed storefronts, followed closely by Metro Hub with 9.

### Where can I find the most hidden supply crates?
Hive Mall has the highest density of hidden crates with 13 collectible supply containers tucked across its multi-level interior walkways.

### How do I travel between distant districts before 2 AM?
You can take the rapid transit trains at the Metro Hub, hail an automated HOVA flying taxi from any roadside stand, or navigate canal waters directly using your motorboat.

## Achievement System Overview & 100% Roadmap

With over 100 Steam achievements, **Nivalis Nights** rewards players for mastering every facet of urban cyberpunk life—from grinding out initial debt payments behind a noodle counter to exploring submerged canal reefs and acquiring luxury penthouses.

## 2. Hospitality & Business Achievements

- **Debt Free**: Completely repay the 50,000 Lims debt inherited from Thaddeus Carmunis.
- **Michelin of the Canals**: Maintain a 5.0-star customer review average across 50 consecutive covers.
- **Passive Automation**: Hire your first Venue Manager at a Level 3 establishment to automate logistics.
- **Nightlife Sovereign**: Generate over 10,000 Credits in net profit during a single night shift at your nightclub.

---

## 3. Master Angler: Fish & Waterway Badges

- **Canal Apprentice**: Catch your first fish from the Seaside Boardwalk docks.
- **Bioluminescent Hunter**: Catch a Glowing Salmon during night hours aboard your motorboat.
- **The Obsidian Prize**: Hook the legendary 0.3% ultra-rare **Obsidian Koi** in deep canal trenches.
- **Grand Angler (Gold)**: Successfully register all 24 physically simulated fish species in your Angler Compendium.

## 4. Real Estate & Exploration Collectibles

- **Capsule Inhabitant**: Purchase your first residential apartment upgrade.
- **Interior Virtuoso**: Place over 100 unique furniture items across your properties from the 620+ catalog.
- **Hive Scavenger**: Discover all 13 hidden supply crates tucked inside Hive Mall.
- **Skyline Penthouse**: Purchase the top-tier luxury penthouse overlooking Central Canyon.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are any achievements missable?
No. Storyline choices affect dialogue flavor and romantic pacing, but all commercial milestones, fish species, and collectibles remain accessible throughout sandbox endgame play.

### Do achievements track across multiple save files?
Collectibles and fish compendium entries are tied to individual save profiles. It is recommended to complete 100% progression on your primary commercial career save.

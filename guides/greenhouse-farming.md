## Automation prerequisites
Update 5 introduced **Automatic Harvester at Farming level 15** and **RePlanter 4000 at level 16**. Check the current requirements and enabled automation for the selected module. The level rebalance can change what an existing save has unlocked.

## Auto-plant not working or storage full?
Update 6 fixes replanting the same crop after an auto-harvest even when greenhouse storage is full. If a module stays empty or changes crop on your updated build:
1. Record the build, module, selected crop, automation state and available storage before harvesting.
2. Observe one harvest and compare the resulting crop and stock.
3. Report the state before and after if it still differs. Do not call the old full-storage bug a permanent game rule.

This is an observation sequence, not a guarantee of recovery. Check your inventory and storage before deleting or replacing a module.

## Why was an old module refunded?
Update 6 refunds the base price of modules locked by Update 5's level changes and returns a growing crop's seed. Inspect the refund message and your current level before buying a replacement; do not interpret every missing module as a lost save.

## Supply your venues
Enable **Use Greenhouse Stock** for the relevant venue. Update 6 restock messages identify whether supply came from the greenhouse, vendors or both. For purchasing and accounting questions, use [manager spending](/guides/manager-money-drain/) and [venue settings](/guides/venue-management-settings/).

## Compare growing with buying
Record a full crop cycle: seed and operating costs, module count, elapsed in-game time and usable harvest. Enter the same units in the [harvest planner](/tools/greenhouse-simulator/). Its estimate follows your inputs, not a hidden verified climate formula. For storage after loading, see [food and recipe fixes](/guides/cuisine-recipes/).

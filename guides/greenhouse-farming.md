## 1. Why Greenhouse Farming is Crucial for Restaurant Margins

Running **Ramen Noir** or any hospitality venue in Nivalis requires an endless stream of fresh ingredients: scallions, shiitake mushrooms, chili peppers, bok choy, and synthetic wheat. In the early game, players are forced to purchase these commodities from street wholesalers or black market couriers at a **180% to 260% markup**.

By unlocking the **District 4 Hydroponic Greenhouse**, you transition from a retail buyer into a self-sustaining food mogul:
- **Zero-Cost Fresh Produce**: Harvested crops automatically route to your kitchen storage, cutting meal preparation cost per bowl from **85 Credits to 18 Credits**.
- **Quality Multipliers**: Grade-A organically grown hydroponic veggies grant a **+25% customer satisfaction tip** on all gourmet noodle dishes.
- **Surplus Trading**: Any overflow crops can be wholesaled directly to rival diners at a guaranteed daily profit margin.

## 2. The Farming Console & Climate Calibration

The greenhouse is powered by a central **Climate Management Console**. Unlike basic farming simulators, crops in Nivalis require precise balancing across three atmospheric parameters:

| Parameter | Optimal Baseline | Impact of Under-Tuning | Impact of Over-Tuning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Temperature** | 21°C – 23°C | Stunted sprout growth (-40% yield) | Heat wilt, thermal blight risk |
| **Humidity** | 68% – 74% | Dry root rot, withered leaves | Mold infestation, fungal rot |
| **Light Spectrum** | 460nm Blue / 660nm Red | Pale leaves, slow photosynthesis | Leaf burn, high electricity bills |

### Calibration Steps
1. Approach the terminal located at the greenhouse entrance.
2. Select the specific planter bay (Bay A, Bay B, or Bay C).
3. Assign the target crop preset based on the strain requirements.
4. Set the **Day/Night Cycle** to 16 hours active LED / 8 hours dark resting to match city grid peak tariffs.

## 4. Automation & Level 3 Greenhouse Managers

Manually watering planter beds and harvesting ripe trays consumes valuable daylight hours that could be spent courting Ava or expanding nightclub VIP sections.

To fully automate the facility:
1. **Install Drip Irrigation Tubing**: Upgrade tier from the Docks Hardware Vendor (Cost: 4,500 Credits).
2. **Deploy Harvest Scraper Drones**: Automatically snips mature stems and deposits them in the freight conveyor.
3. **Hire an Agricultural Specialist**:
   - Recruit **Botanist Jin** at the Midtown Night Market.
   - Wage: 120 Credits / day.
   - Passive Benefit: Prevents fungal blights and automatically recalibrates UV lamps when acid rain drops external city temperatures.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I grow exotic tea leaves for the Nightclub lounge?
Yes. Once your Greenhouse reaches Tier 3, you unlock Hydro-Pod Bays D and E, which allow cultivation of **Caffeine-Fern** and **Mint-Lotus** used exclusively in craft cocktail recipes at your nightclub.

### What happens during city power blackouts?
If the district experiences an emergency brownout (often triggered by corporate strikes), your console battery lasts for 6 in-game hours. Keep an **Auxiliary Fuel Cell** stored in the tool cabinet to prevent crop spoilage.

### Where can I buy rare hybrid seed packets?
Visit **Salt Pete** near the lower canal jetties on Tuesday and Friday nights. He periodically sells confiscated smuggler seeds with +50% yield genetic traits.

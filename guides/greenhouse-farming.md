## Growing is an ingredient source
The game supports growing ingredients. The October 5 update also mentions three new greenhouses in the Industrial District. That establishes their availability in that update, not a climate-control formula.

## Record a harvest cycle
Write down the crop name shown in your game, starting stock, elapsed in-game time and harvested quantity. Repeat under the same conditions before treating a result as typical.

## Compare with purchasing
Count the costs you actually incur and the amount you can use in your venues. The [harvest worksheet](https://nivalis-nights.online/tools/greenhouse-simulator/) averages your observations. It has no temperature, humidity or yield presets and does not predict growth mechanics.

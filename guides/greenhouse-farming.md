## Plan ingredients for the kitchen
Choose ingredients your venues can use and check the available growing space. The October 5 update added three greenhouses in the Industrial District.

## Record one complete cycle
Keep the crop name, rack count, seed and operating costs, elapsed in-game days and total harvest together. Use the same unit for each entry.

## Compare growing with buying
Enter the cycle in the [harvest planner](/tools/greenhouse-simulator/) to estimate supply at a planned rack count and compare its cost with wholesale replacement. Save climate settings as notes alongside the plan.

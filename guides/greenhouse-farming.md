## Plan ingredients for the kitchen
Choose ingredients your venues can use and check the available growing space. The October 5 update added three greenhouses in the Industrial District.

## Record one complete cycle
Keep the crop name, rack count, seed and operating costs, elapsed in-game days and total harvest together. Use the same unit for each entry.

## Compare growing with buying
Enter the cycle in the [harvest planner](/tools/greenhouse-simulator/) to estimate supply at a planned rack count and compare its cost with wholesale replacement. Save climate settings as notes alongside the plan.

## Greenhouse automation in Update 5

Automatic Harvester unlocks at Farming level 15; RePlanter 4000 unlocks at level 16 and replants after harvesting. A venue's Use Greenhouse Stock option makes managers use greenhouse stock before buying ingredients. The update rebalanced farming levels, so check the current level and upgrade requirements on an existing save.

Check automation and venue supply settings before recording a growing cycle on the new version. See [official Update 5](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846651706678000) and the [update summary](/guides/patch-notes/) for the change.

The article does not identify a patch or Steam build. These are attributed media observations, not our playtest or a guarantee for later updates.

## Tier 4 route — media report
The walkthrough links venue progression to level 5 upgrades. It identifies **Azure Breeze on Calypso Island** as the Tier 3 venue to upgrade for the first Tier 4 licence, then names **Fly In (Spire)** and **Obsidian Noir (Metro Hub)** as Tier 4 destinations.

Before spending, inspect the licence requirement on your own venue screen. Budget separately for rent, upgrades and operating costs; this is editorial planning advice. See [business budgeting](/guides/business-expansion/).

## Late story checkpoints — spoilers
The same article covers **Thaddeus Retirement** and **Where Is Ava?**. Its retirement route involves messages and Egress; its Ava route leads through Gan Coilson, a Thera-Pay drive, a Space Port terminal and Egress. It warns that the Ava sequence reaches the ending.

Keep a separate save before following late story objectives. Use the linked walkthrough for the full sequence; this page deliberately gives checkpoints rather than reproducing the article. A day number from one author's run is not a universal quest trigger.

## If your quest does not advance
Editorial checklist: match the exact active journal objective, finish the current conversation, check incoming messages and record your build before reporting a blocker. Do not assume buying a venue forces a story event.

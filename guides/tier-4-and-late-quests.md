## Tier 4 venue route
Start with the Tier 3 venue **Azure Breeze on Calypso Island** and its level 5 upgrade. Check your Tier 4 licence before moving on to **Fly In (Spire)** or **Obsidian Noir (Metro Hub)**.

Before spending, inspect the licence requirement on your own venue screen. Budget separately for rent, upgrades and operating costs. See [business budgeting](/guides/business-expansion/).

## Late story checkpoints — spoilers
The **Thaddeus Retirement** checkpoints involve messages and Egress. **Where Is Ava?** leads through Gan Coilson, a Thera-Pay drive, a Space Port terminal and Egress, reaching the ending.

Keep a separate save before following late story objectives. Follow your current journal one objective at a time. If the next step does not appear, check conversations and incoming messages.

## If your quest does not advance
Match the exact active journal objective, finish the current conversation, check incoming messages and record your build before reporting a blocker. Do not assume buying a venue forces a story event.

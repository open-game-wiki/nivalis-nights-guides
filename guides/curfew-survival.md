## 2. Drone Searchlights & Violation Penalties

At **02:00 sharp**, city klaxons sound across all districts. Automated Corporate Security Drones deploy from rooftop hangars and patrol standard pedestrian bridges, highways, and public plazas.

### What Happens If You Get Caught
1. **Searchlight Cone**: Drones project an amber conical spotlight on the ground. If you walk into it, the spotlight shifts to piercing red, and a 3-second lock-on siren sounds.
2. **Municipal Violation Fine**: Getting scanned slaps your balance with an immediate **1,500 Credit fine**. If you do not have enough cash, your debt balance automatically increases with compounding daily interest.
3. **Inventory Confiscation**: Any contraband items (such as unlicensed fish, black market electronics, or hacked access chips) in your backpack are permanently confiscated.
4. **Fatigue Collapse**: If your character stays awake past **04:00**, a fatigue meter drains your stamina to zero. You will collapse unconscious, waking up at 09:00 in an emergency med-bay with a **2,000 Credit medical bill** and an empty inventory.

## 3. Proven Shelters & Bed Anchors

To avoid curfew fines and fatigue debuffs, players must anchor themselves in verified rest points before 02:00:

### 1. Personal Motorboat Bunk
One of the best early-game investments is adding a **Fold-Out Sleeper Bunk** to your canal motorboat. As long as your boat is anchored under low canal overhangs away from open harbor patrol lines, sleeping in your boat fulfills the daily rest requirement at **zero rental cost**.

### 2. District Rental Apartments
Owning or renting an apartment in each major sector (Midtown, Docks, Cloud Level) gives you convenient proximity anchors. When sirens sound at 01:45, simply dash into the nearest apartment lobby elevator.

### 3. Covered Alleys & Ventilation Shafts
If you are caught far from home, seek refuge under the low corrugated tin awnings of the lower under-slums. Drones cannot scan beneath dense pipe networks and overhead cable clusters.

### 4. Scanner Scrambler Hardware
Purchasing a **Pocket Radio Jammer** from hacker NPC **Salma** grants you two temporary scan-negation charges per night, giving you an emergency 10-second escape window if illuminated by searchlights.

While curfew poses lethal financial risks, running the streets between 02:00 and 05:00 unlocks the highest profit margins in the game:

* **Black Market Procurement**: Smuggler barges only dock at Lower Sluice Gate 7 between 02:30 and 04:00. Rare liquor crates and contraband truffle seeds can be purchased at a **70% discount**.
* **Midnight Trophy Angling**: The legendary 0.3% rare species—such as the **Obsidian Koi** and **Trench Leviathan Minnow**—only spawn in city canals during total curfew darkness.
* **Secret Speakeasy VIPs**: High-net-worth corporate fugitives frequent underground backroom bars during curfew hours, offering lucrative side-quests and high-paying investor contracts.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can drones track me inside my motorboat while cruising?
Only if you travel at full throttle under open skyways. Cruising at half throttle with navigation lights turned off under lower archways keeps your heat signature below drone detection thresholds.

### Does drinking espresso prevent fatigue collapse?
Consuming double-shot espresso or energy stimulants temporarily staves off fatigue collapse for 60 in-game minutes, buying you crucial time to reach a safe bed anchor.

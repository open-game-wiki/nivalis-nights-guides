## Does Nivalis Nights have multiplayer or co-op?
The developer FAQ describes Nivalis Nights as **single-player**, with no multiplayer or co-op features planned. Meeting characters, managing relationships and visiting their spaces are NPC systems, not player-to-player networking.

## PS5, Xbox, Mac and Linux
The same FAQ says PC is the current focus and does not announce a console date. It also lists no native Mac or Linux version. A handheld compatibility layer or a community mod is different from an announced native port.

Before purchasing for a particular device, check the [current Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1488490/Nivalis_Nights/) and official announcements. Do not use a store bundle, controller support label or Cloudpunk's platform list as evidence that Nivalis Nights has the same releases.

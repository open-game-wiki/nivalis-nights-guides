## City Navigation & Transit System Overview

The multi-tiered vertical architecture of **Nivalis** means walking everywhere is impossible if you need to manage restaurants in Meridian Market while maintaining hydroponic greenhouses in the Industrial District. Understanding transit networks saves vital hours before the 2 AM curfew sirens sound.

---

## 1. Waterway Navigation: Your Personal Boat

Unlocked after completing introductory storyline quests with Ava at the Seaside Boardwalk, your motorboat is the ultimate personal transit asset:
- **Free Movement**: No tolls or fuel costs.
- **Access to Offshore Reefs**: Reaches sunken ruins, canal junctions, and offshore islands inaccessible by foot.
- **The Floating Safehouse**: Features an onboard fold-out bunk allowing you to sleep anywhere in quiet waters, completely bypassing street-level curfew enforcement.

---

## 2. HOVA Aerial Taxis: Instant Fast-Travel

Hover-cabs operate from dedicated rooftop and promenade landing pads across all major districts:
- **Calling a Cab**: Approach any glowing yellow HOVA terminal to summon a taxi.
- **Instant Transit**: Instantly transports you between connected sectors.
- **Emergency Lifesaver**: If it is 01:45 AM and you are stranded in Central Canyon far from your bed, taking a HOVA taxi guarantees reaching your home apartment before sirens sound at 02:00.

---

## 3. Sky Monorails & The Metro Hub

The city's rapid rail transit radiates from the massive **Metro Hub**:
- **Affordable Bulk Travel**: At only 5 Credits per ticket, monorails are the cheapest way to travel between industrial greenhouse plots and downtown markets.
- **Operating Hours**: Trains run from 05:30 AM until 01:30 AM. After 01:30 AM, station gates shutter in preparation for the martial curfew.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can HOVA taxis crash or get delayed by weather?
No, HOVA taxis use automated navigation corridors and function normally even during heavy torrential acid rain downpours.

### Can I upgrade my personal motorboat?
Yes, Ava's dock workshop sells motor enhancements, expanded onboard ice coolers for fish storage, and decorative cabin lighting.

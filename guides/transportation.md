## Available ways to travel
Choose between walking, public transit, taxi services and boats for your destination. Check the available route and any displayed fare before setting out.

## Plan a trip around an objective
Record your departure landmark, destination and the route the game offers. If a fare is displayed, check it at the time of travel rather than assuming a price from an old guide.

## Performance after travelling
The October 2 update documented a performance issue after fast travel to some locations, with saving and reloading as a workaround. This was a dated known issue, not a claim that every current build still needs that step. Check newer patch notes first.

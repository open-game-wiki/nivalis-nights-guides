## Support and certification are different
The September 29 launch announcement said full Steam Deck support was not available at launch. The official known-issues post lists Deck performance work. These are dated statements; they do not establish the current Valve compatibility rating. Check the live Steam listing before purchasing for a handheld.

## No measured preset is published here
We do not have a reproducible LCD/OLED benchmark for the current build. Claims of locked 60 FPS, tested battery life and fixed TDP settings have been removed. A frame cap is a limit, not evidence that the game sustains that rate.

## How to compare your own settings
Record the Deck model, SteamOS/Proton versions, game build, resolution and settings. Test the same route and a crowded location, noting frame-time variation and battery draw. Change one setting at a time; keep your original configuration so you can compare it. Report the device and reproduction steps when contacting support.

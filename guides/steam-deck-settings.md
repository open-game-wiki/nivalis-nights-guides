## Check compatibility and updates
Check the current Steam compatibility rating and update the game before comparing settings. Launch-era support statements may differ from the version now installed.

## Compare the same route
Note your LCD or OLED model, SteamOS/Proton version, resolution and frame cap. Use a familiar route and a crowded area to compare frame pacing and battery use.

## Keep a usable profile
Change one setting at a time and keep the original profile for comparison. A frame cap sets the upper limit; check whether the game holds it during movement and busy scenes.

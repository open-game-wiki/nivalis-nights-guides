## What to revisit after updating
Open venue settings and check restock levels, daily budgets and greenhouse stock options. Compare operating records separately before and after an update. Then inspect your current quest, inventory and whether saves load correctly.

See [greenhouse farming](/guides/greenhouse-farming/) for automation, [venue settings](/guides/venue-management-settings/) for purchasing, and [performance troubleshooting](/guides/performance-troubleshooting/) for crashes and stutter. Each update link leads to the full official change list; the table highlights changes relevant to using these guides.


## Update 6 · October 22, 2026
This release addresses Ava, Network and Aseptic progression, greenhouse replanting, venue supply/accounting, service during sleep and food storage. It also includes long-session memory and controller improvements, not a fixed FPS guarantee.

- [Manager spending](/guides/manager-money-drain/): inspect source messages and cash movement.
- [Greenhouses](/guides/greenhouse-farming/): check full-storage replanting and level-change refunds.
- [Food and recipes](/guides/cuisine-recipes/): revisit fridges after loading, Beer Soup and Squid Ink.

The announcement still lists incomplete translations and incomplete player drink cleanup, with staff cleanup available. A fix promised for the next patch is not a released fix in this one. [Official Update 6](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1488490/announcements/?l=english), checked October 28, 2026. Compare historical workarounds with this version before using them.

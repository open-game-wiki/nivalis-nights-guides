## Start with a short, familiar route
Use the same quiet route to compare settings. Note the current camera sensitivity, frame pacing and display mode. If motion is uncomfortable, pause the test rather than pushing through it.

## Adjust one option at a time
Inspect the controls available in your installed build. Try smaller changes in camera sensitivity and compare stable frame-rate limits. Record which option changed and how the route felt.

## Keep a usable profile
Write down the settings that felt comfortable along with your resolution, device and build. For performance questions, consult [PC requirements](/guides/system-requirements/) and [Steam Deck notes](/guides/steam-deck-settings/).

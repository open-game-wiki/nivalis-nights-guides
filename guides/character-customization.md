## Character Presentation & First-Person Identity

In **Nivalis Nights**, developer ION LANDS deliberately chose a **true first-person perspective** to deliver unprecedented immersion into their voxel cyberpunk metropolis. Rather than viewing an avatar from behind, you inhabit your character directly—experiencing the neon drizzle, kitchen steam, and canal waves through your own eyes.

---

## 1. True First-Person & Visible Body Mechanics

- **Physical Body Presence**: Looking down reveals your character's legs, torso, footwear, and active clothing.
- **Interactive Hands & Forearms**: Because your hands are constantly visible while piloting boats, cooking ramen, and shaking cocktails, cosmetic customization heavily prioritizes arm-level aesthetics:
  - **Cybernetic Prosthetic Sleeves**: Replace organic forearms with chrome, carbon-fiber, or glowing neon cyberware.
  - **Tactical Rings & Smartwatches**: Display in-game clock, district alerts, and currency counters directly on your wrist.
  - **Gloves & Sleeves**: From heavy-duty kitchen oven mitts to sleek leather jackets purchased from boutique tailors.

## 3. Apartment Mirrors & Vanity Grooming

Inside your residential apartments, interacting with the **Bathroom Vanity Mirror** opens your personal grooming interface:
- **Hairstyles & Neon Dyes**: Switch hair color between vibrant cyan, neon magenta, acid green, or matte black.
- **Facial Cyberware**: Equip optical implants, facial subdermal seams, and holographic cheek tattoos visible during mirror interactions and NPC dialogue portraits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a third-person camera option in Nivalis Nights?
No. The developer specifically designed *Nivalis Nights* exclusively from a true first-person viewpoint to maximize atmospheric immersion and spatial intimacy.

### Do clothes grant stat buffs or business bonuses?
Certain outfits provide minor environmental perks—such as heavy rain slickers reducing stamina drain during torrential storms, or formal blazers granting a minor tip bonus in upscale nightclubs.

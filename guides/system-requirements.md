## 3 Critical Hardware Takeaways

### 1. 16 GB System Memory is a Strict Baseline
Many indie simulation games manage with 8 GB of RAM, but *Nivalis Nights* simulates dozens of persistent background schedules: over 130 voiced characters moving along urban routines, active customer pathfinding inside your venues, and climate simulations across rented greenhouses. Running below 16 GB will cause stuttering during district transitions.

### 2. VRAM Matters More Than Raw Compute
The game features 620+ unique furniture items, 240+ ingredients, and dense neon lighting. Texture buffers and voxel geometry caching fill video memory rapidly. Cards with 4 GB VRAM are not supported; you need at least **6 GB VRAM** to prevent texture pop-in and out-of-memory errors.

### 3. Mechanical Hard Drives (HDDs) are Unsupported
Because the city streams seamless vertical layers from lower water canals to upper residential spires without loading screens, an **SSD is strictly mandatory**. Installing on a spinning HDD will cause asset streaming freezes and falling through geometry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I run Nivalis Nights on a gaming laptop?
Yes. An RTX 4050 Laptop (6 GB) handles Minimum/Medium settings comfortably, while an RTX 4060 Laptop (8 GB) is recommended for 1080p 60 FPS smooth gameplay.

### Does the game support DLSS or FSR?
The official benchmarks were tested at native resolution. DLSS, FSR, and XeSS upscaling features can be enabled to gain an additional 20–35% frame rate buffer on modern graphics cards.

## Common controls

Confirm your current bindings in Settings → Controls; custom bindings may differ.

| Action | Keyboard | Xbox | PlayStation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | WASD | Left stick | Left stick |
| Sprint | Shift | L3 | L3 |
| Interact | E | A | × |
| Placement mode | P | X | □ |
| Pick up | X | Y | △ |
| Scan | C | R3 | R3 |
| Map | M | D-pad up | D-pad up |
| Inventory | I | D-pad right | D-pad right |
| Journal | J | D-pad left | D-pad left |
| Shopping list | L | D-pad down | D-pad down |

## Before fishing

Equip a rod through your inventory and follow the current on-screen casting and reeling prompts. Check bindings again after adding mods.

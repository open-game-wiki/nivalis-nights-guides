## Prepare the next venue
Review existing venues before taking on another restaurant or nightclub. Keep cash available for staff, ingredients and fixed costs as well as the new purchase.

## Review restocking controls
Update #4 on October 9 added restocking targets and daily budgets. Review both controls together and compare the shopping list with your active menu.

## Compare the costs
Separate initial investment from recurring costs in the [profit planner](/tools/business-calculator/). For your next licence, continue with [Tier 4 venues and late quests](/guides/tier-4-and-late-quests/).

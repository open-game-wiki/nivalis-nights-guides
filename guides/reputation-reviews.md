## Customer Reviews & Hospitality Growth Overview

In **Nivalis**, culinary excellence alone will not guarantee a packed dining room. Developer ION LANDS has engineered an authentic hospitality loop where customer reviews actively dictate patron foot traffic, welcoming interior design directly stimulates trade, and players can monetize their decorative flair across the metropolis through freelance design contracts.

---

## 1. Customer Reviews: The Primary Growth Engine

In official gameplay showcases, the developer highlights:
> *"Manage your menus across the city — make sure customers are happy and leave good reviews."*

Good reviews generate organic social buzz, drawing higher pedestrian volumes from surrounding transit terminals and retail promenades into your venues. Customer satisfaction is dictated by three operational pillars:

1. **Service Speed**:
   - Ensuring food preparation stations and serving counters process orders promptly before patron patience expires.
2. **Ingredient Freshness (Update #2)**:
   - Serving aged inventory via FIFO consumption lowers your *"Food Freshness"* review sub-score. Running out of ingredients mid-service inflicts severe reputation penalties.
3. **Atmospheric Comfort**:
   - Providing sufficient seating, attractive lighting, and stylish fixtures encourages diners to linger, order second drinks, and leave higher tips.

---

## 2. Decor Buffs: How 620+ Furniture Items Affect Trade

Unlike standard simulation games where furniture is merely an aesthetic vanity, interior decoration in Nivalis directly affects your business bottom line:
> *"Decorate your places to make them feel welcoming... with over 620 furniture items to collect and place."*

A sterile concrete noodle bar with bare countertops will experience lower footfall and weaker customer retention compared to a thoughtfully appointed dining room. Operators can leverage decor across three key areas:

### Lighting & Ambiance
- **Neon Wall Fixtures & Paper Lanterns**: Elevate venue warmth and nighttime aesthetic appeal, granting an immediate +15% ambiance multiplier during evening dinner rushes.

### Seating Capacity & Layout
- **Booths, Barstools & Dining Tables**: Adding comfortable booths increases simultaneous covers, allowing more patrons to dine during peak hours without overcrowding bottlenecks.

### Prestige Accents
- **Planters, Wall Art & Glassware Cabinets**: Premium decorative pieces raise venue prestige tiers, directly unlocking higher maximum menu pricing thresholds.

### Where to Buy Furniture & Decor
- **Eastern Residential**: Quaint retro residential furniture and cozy domestic lighting.
- **Hive Mall**: Modern boutique appliances, sleek barstools, and electronic display signs.
- **Central Canyon**: Luxury executive lounge fixtures, mahogany counters, and rare art pieces.

---

## 3. Freelance Interior Design: Early Cash Flow

If your capital reserves dip low during early gameplay, you can monetize your decorating skills:

- **Finding Clients**: Speak with apartment owners and small shopkeepers in **Meridian Market** looking for refurbishments.
- **Contract Fulfillment**: Arrange furniture, select color palettes, and place lighting to meet the client's design brief.
- **Financial Reward**: Completing a single interior contract nets **300 to 600 Credits**, providing immediate cash for greenhouse seeds or commercial refrigerator upgrades.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does placing random furniture everywhere boost venue ratings?
No. Decor buffs require balanced spacing and functional layouts. Crowding paths blocks employee navigation and slows service times, hurting your review score.

### Can bad reviews cause a venue to go out of business?
While your venue will not instantly close down, severe negative reviews drop foot traffic dramatically, leading to negative daily cash flow if staff wages exceed sales revenue.

### Where is the best place to buy starter plants and lighting?
Hive Mall and Eastern Residential offer affordable introductory plants and warm ambient neon lamps suitable for starter stalls like Ramen Noir.

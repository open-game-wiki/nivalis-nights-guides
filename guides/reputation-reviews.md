## Read the complaint in context
Treat a customer review as an observation about that visit. Write down the complaint and the venue conditions before changing several things at once.

## Freshness changed in an update
The October 2 patch made older ingredients get used first. The developer explains the tradeoff: less spoiled stock can also mean a lower freshness component in customer reviews. A freshness complaint therefore needs an inventory check, not only new decoration.

## Make one change at a time
Compare a similar service period after altering staffing, stock or layout. Record what changed; this helps distinguish a repeatable effect from a different mix of customers.

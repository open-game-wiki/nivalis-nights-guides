## A record of your catches
Fishing is a confirmed activity, but the official sources reviewed here do not establish a complete species count or a rarity table. This page therefore does not claim to list every fish.

## Useful fields to collect
Record the displayed fish name, where it was caught, the equipment used, in-game time and game version. A screenshot preserves spelling and distinguishes an item name from a nickname.

## What a catch cannot prove
One catch in rain does not establish a rain requirement. A short run without a fish does not establish its drop rate. Keep observations separate from inferred rules. The [personal catch notebook](https://nivalis-nights.online/tools/fish-compendium/) starts empty and is for your own records.

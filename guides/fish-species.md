## Pick a target before travelling
Open the [fish compendium](/tools/fish-compendium/) and select your district, current weather and daytime or night. Fish without those restrictions remain in the results. Choose “Not caught” to plan around missing species. See the [fishing spots guide](/guides/fishing-spots/) for district routes.

Exact coordinates, bait requirements and water depths are not established by this table. Do not buy an alleged special bait on its account. Add equipment, game build and observed conditions to your personal records after a catch.

## After the catch
The developer FAQ confirms that fish can supply restaurants or be sold. Compare those options with your observed sale price and kitchen costs in the trip calculator below the compendium. Checking a fish records browser progress; it does not change or synchronize Steam achievements.

## Older catches
The current index uses actual game species. Older checks remain in browser storage and can be recovered as historical personal records. Historical entries are not guessed into a different species.

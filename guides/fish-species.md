## 1. Water Depth Zones & Habitat Distribution

The vertical marine topography of Nivalis is stratified into four distinct environmental columns. To catch specific species, you must equip the appropriate **Line Weight**, **Sinkers**, and **Sonar Sensor Modules** on your fishing rod.

### Tier 1: Shallow Surface (0m – 5m)
* **Locations**: Upper Midtown Boardwalks, Residential Canals, Diner Docks.
* **Common Catches**: Neon Guppy, Canal Mullet, Cyber-Minnow, Silver Anchovy.
* **Tackle Needed**: Basic Carbon Rod with Floating Bobber.
* **Commercial Value**: 8 to 25 Credits. Primarily used for standard fish cakes and starter broth base.

### Tier 2: Mid-Channel Currents (5m – 15m)
* **Locations**: Industrial Transit Aqueducts, Underpass Jetties, Old City Locks.
* **Common Catches**: Chrome Bass, Smog Perch, Bio-Luminescent Tilapia, Copper Trout.
* **Tackle Needed**: Weighted Sinker Level 2, Synthetic Worm Bait.
* **Commercial Value**: 35 to 80 Credits. Essential for mid-tier Ramen Noir seafood bowls.

### Tier 3: Sub-Level Trenches (15m – 30m)
* **Locations**: Lower Slums Under-Canals, Submerged Rail Tunnels, Garbage Chute Outlets.
* **Common Catches**: Acid Eel, Sludge Catfish, Armored Pike, Barbed Glow-Sturgeon.
* **Tackle Needed**: Heavy Tungsten Jig, Phosphor Glow Lure.
* **Commercial Value**: 120 to 300 Credits. High demand in luxury nightlife VIP sashimi platters.

### Tier 4: Harbor Abyssal Trenches (30m+)
* **Locations**: The Deep Ocean Wall, Outer Harbor Warning Buoy, Sunken Cargo Submarine Wreck.
* **Common Catches**: Deep Abyssal Angler, Trench Shark, Kraken Squidling, Void Ray.
* **Tackle Needed**: Deep-Sea Titanium Reel, Reinforced Carbon Fiber Line, Hydro-Sonar Sonobuoy.
* **Commercial Value**: 500 to 1,800 Credits.

## 3. Bait Types & Salt Pete Supplier Synergy

**Salt Pete** runs an unlicensed tackle skiff moored beneath the Central Transit Bridge. Visiting him before 22:00 allows you to stock up on specialized bait unavailable in general stores:

* **Synthetic Paste Bait (5 Cr)**: Attracts Tier 1 shallow feeders. Zero spoil rate.
* **Phosphor Grub (25 Cr)**: Emits a faint green glow underwater, granting a +40% strike rate during nighttime fishing sessions.
* **Smuggler's Pheromone Lure (90 Cr)**: Triples the appearance rate of Tier 3 and Tier 4 predatory trench species for 15 in-game minutes.
* **Bait Synergy Perk**: Once you reach Rank 4 Friendship with Salt Pete, he crafts custom lead lures that prevent line breakage during high-tension strikes.

## 4. Kitchen Integration: Supplying Ramen Noir

While selling your catch directly to dock merchants gives immediate cash in hand, delivering fresh fish directly to **Ramen Noir** yields substantially higher returns:

- **Dock Merchant Sale**: Flat cash payout immediately (e.g., 200 Credits for an Armored Pike).
- **Ramen Noir Kitchen Processing**: One Armored Pike yields **4 portions of Smoked Pike Chashu**. At 95 Credits per premium bowl, the gross yield is **380 Credits (+90% value uplift)**.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I fish while riding inside my motorized Hoverboat?
Yes. Turn off the hoverboat propulsion thrusters and anchor the craft in open water. Fishing from the boat deck gives you access to the deep outer harbor trenches inaccessible from pedestrian walkways.

### How do I prevent fishing line snaps on trophy fish?
Tap the reel button rhythmically rather than holding it down continuously. When the rod tip turns bright red, immediately release tension to allow the fish to run before reeling again when the indicator cools to amber.

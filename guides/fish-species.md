## Find your next fish
Open the [fish compendium](/tools/fish-compendium/) to browse 24 entries. Search a name, bait or recipe, then narrow the list by depth and rarity.

## Prepare for the trip
Check your target’s bait, catch window and recipe information. Pair it with a [fishing route note](/guides/fishing-spots/) so you can return to the same spot.

## Keep your catch checklist
Mark catches in the compendium and filter for fish still missing. Use personal records for locations, equipment and fishing notes, and export those records for backup. Progress stays in this browser.

## Choose a target
Use the [fish compendium](/tools/fish-compendium/) to check bait and depth information before setting out. Take the equipment you intend to compare across trips.

## Remember the location
Note a recognizable landmark and the route from a transit stop or venue. Add a screenshot and whether you fished from shore or aboard a boat.

## Compare return visits
Keep time, bait, equipment and catches together in your personal records. Revisit with similar equipment to compare results and decide where to go next.

## Four districts to plan around
Community records place the restricted species in four districts. Pick one you can already reach, then plan its daytime and night targets around the weather.

| District | Day targets | Night targets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industrial District | Roach, Ruffe; Salmon in sun | Rudd, Sabrefish, Rainbow Trout; Salmon in sun |
| Seaside Boardwalk | Crucian, Bream | Carp, Brook Trout |
| The Stacks | Volga Zander, Zander, Taimen | Tench, Zope, Whitefish |
| Calypso Island | Sunny: Lake Trout, Grayling | Sunny: Ide, Dace, Perch |

Burbot, Pike and Stickleback have no district, weather or time restriction in the guide. Match weather for every other target: this overview does not mean all weather works. [Open the full conditions table](/guides/fish-species/).

## A sunny trip
All five Calypso Island targets are listed for sun. Try Lake Trout and Grayling during the day, then Ide, Dace and Perch at night. If the island is unavailable, try Salmon in Industrial District. The Stacks offers Volga Zander and Zander by day, then Tench, Zope and Whitefish at night in sun.

## Rain and storms
At Seaside Boardwalk in rain, try Crucian by day and Brook Trout at night. In storms, both Carp and Brook Trout are night targets there. Industrial District lists Ruffe by day and Rainbow Trout at night in rain, fog or storms.

## Clouds, dust and fog
Seaside Boardwalk lists Bream by day in clouds, dust or fog, and Carp at night in clouds, dust or storms. Industrial District lists Roach by day and Sabrefish at night in sun, fog or clouds. Check the weather before deciding to wait for night.

## Finding a usable spot
These are district conditions, not verified dock coordinates or turn-by-turn walking directions. Look for an available fishing interaction after arriving. On your first catch, note the transit stop, shore landmark or boat position in your [personal records](/tools/fish-compendium/#personal-catch-records). Choose routes within the districts your save has unlocked.

## 1. Physically Simulated Fish: Not a Dice Roll

Unlike traditional life simulators where fishing is a random loot table triggered by pressing a button at a ripple, **Nivalis Nights** features a physically simulated marine ecosystem:

- **1,000+ Individual Fish in the Water**: Every fish swimming beneath the neon-lit water column exists as a physical entity with individual AI and custom water shaders.
- **What You See Is What You Catch**: If you see a bioluminescent silhouette shimmering green near the canal pylons, targeting that specific outline with your cast determines your catch.
- **Water Column Depth (0m–30m+)**: Different species populate distinct depths. Surface swimmers like Silver Sprats swim near the canal walkways, while deep Trench Leviathans dwell in dark lower sea-level locks.

## 2. Boating & Pier Fishing Locations

You can angle from two distinct platforms:

### Pier & Walkway Spots
- **Seaside Boardwalk**: Ideal for daytime starter catches (Canal Herring, Neon Mullet).
- **Lower Sluice Gates**: Dangerous during curfew, but yields higher-value predatory fish.

### Your Personal Motorboat
- **Mobility Across Canals**: Unlocked via early quests with Ava, your motorboat allows you to reach offshore reef clusters, sunken highway arches, and isolated canal junctions away from streetlights.
- **The Boat is Also a Bed**: You can fish through the evening, anchor under low concrete overpasses, and sleep safely inside your motorboat bunk without triggering 2 AM curfew penalties.

## 3. Night Fishing & The Bioluminescent Glow

Nighttime dramatically alters canal visibility:
- **Glowing Shaders**: Between 20:00 and 04:00, bioluminescent species emit vibrant blues, cyans, and emerald greens beneath the dark waters.
- **Phosphor Bait**: Equipping glow-worms or phosphor lures attracts nocturnal species from deeper trenches to the surface.
- **High-Value Nocturnal Catches**: Catching species like the **Glowing Salmon** or **Obsidian Koi** provides rare culinary ingredients that cannot be purchased from standard daytime market merchants.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I fish during the 2 AM curfew without getting fined?
Yes, as long as you are aboard your motorboat in canal waters away from direct drone patrol spotlights on public bridges.

### Do fish spoil if I keep them in my backpack?
Fish keep until the end of the day, but will spoil by morning unless stored in a refrigerated kitchen storage unit or an onboard ice chest.

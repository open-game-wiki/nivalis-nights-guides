## Boats and fishing
The official description includes exploring by boat and fishing. It does not specify the depth bands, sonar upgrades or rare-spawn coordinates previously listed here.

## Record a location precisely
Use a recognizable landmark and the route taken from a transit stop or venue. Include a screenshot and whether you were on foot or aboard a boat. That makes a location more reproducible than an invented coordinate.

## Compare visits
Repeat visits with the same equipment and note the conditions. An empty visit is useful evidence too, but it is not proof that a location stops producing fish at a specific time. Pair your route log with the [catch notebook](https://nivalis-nights.online/tools/fish-compendium/).

## Choose a first priority
Nivalis Nights combines business management with exploration and relationships. Our suggested starting order is to follow the current tutorial objective, learn your first venue's interface, then explore a nearby route. This is an editorial checklist, not a timed walkthrough.

## Before buying more
Check what an objective actually requests and what is already in venue storage. Record purchases and sales before deciding whether to expand. A busy shop does not by itself prove that it is profitable.

## Build a useful routine
Keep a note of the next objective, the ingredient or equipment it needs, and where you found it. For a specific blocker, consult the [first-session checklist](https://nivalis-nights.online/guides/first-hour-guide/) or [staff notes](https://nivalis-nights.online/guides/staff-management/).

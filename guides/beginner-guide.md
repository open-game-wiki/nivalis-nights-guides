## 5 Core Rules for New Nivalis Tycoons

Welcome to the floating vertical metropolis of Nivalis. Taking your first steps into this neon-drenched cyberpunk city can feel overwhelming between managing kitchen inventory, balancing commercial books, and racing against nocturnal curfews. Follow these five core operational tenets:

1. **Start at Ramen Noir**: Take over the modest street noodle stand left by veteran restaurateur Thaddeus Carmunis at canal level. Keep your starter menu lean and high-margin.
2. **Connect with Ava Early**: Meet your primary companion Ava at the Seaside Boardwalk to unlock vital dock connections, fishing spots, and wholesale merchant introductions.
3. **The 3 Operational Pillars**: Every dish requires **Recipes, fresh Ingredients, and adequate Kitchen Equipment**. Prioritize upgrading stove capacity and refrigeration before buying luxury cosmetic furniture.
4. **Capitalize on Rush Hours**: Prep ingredients before 11:30 AM for the Lunch Rush; shift to higher-priced dinner offerings during the 18:00–21:30 Evening Rush.
5. **Respect the 2:00 AM Curfew**: Close shop by 01:00 AM, deposit cash into terminals, and return to your residence before martial curfew sirens sound across the city.

High-end dishes demand high-grade ingredients. Sourcing fresh canal fish by boat and growing organic greens in rented Industrial greenhouses beats paying retail prices at street stalls.

- **Wholesale Sourcing**: Visit wholesale merchants in Meridian Market every morning before the 11:30 AM lunch rush. Buying dry noodles, broth concentrate, and scallions in bulk saves up to 40% compared to last-minute retail runs.
- **Canal Fishing**: Once Ava introduces you to dock navigation, canal fishing provides 100% margin fresh seafood for your soup specials.
- **Greenhouse Production**: Renting hydroponic greenhouse beds in the Industrial District lets you automate the production of scallions, ginger, radishes, and wasabi under controlled climate conditions.

## 02 / Reputation & Customer Reviews

In Nivalis, patron reviews drive organic foot traffic. Satisfied customers leave positive feedback that boosts your stand's reputation across the district:

- **Service Speed**: Food prep stations and serving counters must process orders promptly before patron patience expires.
- **Atmospheric Comfort**: Adding comfortable stools and warm lighting increases covers and boosts average tips.
- **Menu Balance**: Don't price your starting noodles too high until your kitchen equipment can reliably produce 4-star dish quality.

---

## 03 / Midnight Survival & The 2 AM Curfew

Never let greed risk your personal safety. Trying to squeeze one more bowl of ramen at 01:45 AM will get you caught in curfew lockdowns:

- **The Sirens Sound at 02:00 AM**: Emergency sirens echo across canals and catwalks.
- **Drone Enforcement**: Patrolling security drones target citizens caught on outdoor streets.
- **The Penalty**: Getting caught drains stamina, causes hospital collapse, and incurs a painful **1,200 Credits fine** along with lost inventory.
- **Safe Havens**: Your apartment bed is the safest refuge. If you're caught far out on the water, anchoring your motorboat in quiet canal water also allows you to sleep safely through the curfew.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the very first thing you should do in Nivalis Nights?
Begin by inspecting your starter noodle stand, Ramen Noir, left behind by Thaddeus. Review your initial inventory of dry noodles and basic spices, and connect with companion Ava at the Seaside Boardwalk docks to unlock fishing and ingredient suppliers.

### How do you avoid running out of ingredients during rush hour?
Visit wholesale merchants in Meridian Market every morning before the 11:30 AM lunch rush, or rent a greenhouse in the Industrial District to maintain a steady harvest of fresh produce.

### When should you close your business for the night?
Close your doors and settle customer checks by 01:00 AM. This provides ample time to deposit your cash earnings and catch a HOVA taxi or Metro train to reach your apartment before the 02:00 AM curfew.

### What should you invest your first profits into?
Prioritize kitchen appliance upgrades (higher quality cooking stoves and refrigerated storage) to unlock higher-margin recipes and prevent ingredient spoilage before buying cosmetic apartment decorations.

## Start with the tutorial
Follow the current tutorial objective and learn the first venue’s menu, stock and staff screens. Once the basics feel familiar, explore a nearby route.

## Check before buying
Read the requested item, quantity and destination. Check existing stock before making another purchase, and compare sales with ingredient spending and wages before expanding.

## Find the next guide
Use the [first-session checklist](/guides/first-hour-guide/) for tutorial blockers and [staff management](/guides/staff-management/) when orders stall. Keep your next objective and needed supplies together.

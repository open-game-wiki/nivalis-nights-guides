## First meeting
By a railing in the Sewers.

## Plan your romance order
Kenzy and Reena need you to have no partner at the confession and exclude one another on a single save. Finish your choice before completing Deeala, Zaffer, Cale or Mia. Garnet does not count towards this restriction.

This route counts towards the partner restriction. Decide your order before accepting Mia’s apartment invitation. [Compare all seven routes and their order](/guides/romance-ava/)

<h2 id="romance-mia-jay">Mia Jay</h2>

**Trigger and preparation：** Unlock the Sewers with 30 achievement points, collect any seven of thirteen quest k20 Tumblers, and reach romance ≥2 before the apartment invitation.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Mia Jay](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Sewers railing**

   **Wait：** After reaching the Sewers; no extra day wait is stated.

   **Dialogue and action：** Offer to collect the tumblers. Light teasing followed by explaining that you were joking gives +1; asking about voyeurism is another +1.

   **Condition and branch：** Thirst Trap places thirteen tumblers; you only need seven.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Mia Jay · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

2. **Any seven tumbler districts**

   **Wait：** No fixed waiting interval is reported; first collect seven.

   **Dialogue and action：** Early options are Lowtown, Meridian Market, Docks, Oil Rig, Eastern Residential, Metro Hub and Sewers.

   **Condition and branch：** These are seven alternative pickup locations, not a requirement to gather all thirteen or unlock Calypso Island.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Mia Jay · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Mia Jay quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

3. **0 collected: Sewers; 1–5: Metro Hub; 6: Skyhigh Gardens recycling bins**

   **Wait：** Optional progress conversations while collecting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Support her interests (+2) or speak of the ghost as something shared (+1). At six tumblers, emphasise that it matters to her to set romance to 5. Saying you only want to help resets romance to 0.

   **Condition and branch：** Skyhigh Gardens requires 90 points, but this optional six-tumbler chat is not required to turn in seven at Ramen Noir.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Mia Jay · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

4. **Ramen Noir**

   **Wait：** After collecting seven; no fixed day delay is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** At delivery say the effort was for her (+1). Accept her invitation request only when ready to complete this romance. To keep Kenzy/Reena open, encourage her to develop her own ideas instead. The apartment acceptance is “You're welcome any time.”

   **Condition and branch：** Romance ≥2 enables the invitation. Delivery rewards 1,000 Lims and Ruby Red even if you decline the visit.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Mia Jay · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Mia Jay quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

5. **Your apartment**

   **Wait：** That evening, 20:00–03:00 across midnight.

   **Dialogue and action：** Attend the apartment scene; after the night skip Ava plays Mia’s message.

   **Condition and branch：** Accepting the invitation commits this ending that night, even if you later turn her away at the door. Find Love completes.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Mia Jay · step 5](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

**Missable branches：** Do not invite Mia home before finishing a desired Kenzy/Reena route. The seven-tumbler delivery is separate from optional progress chats, and Love Den is a later design job rather than the first romance trigger.

**Completion：** 1,000 Lims, Ruby Red and Find Love.

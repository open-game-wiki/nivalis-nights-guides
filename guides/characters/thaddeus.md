## Meet Thaddeus in Meridian Market
The opening conversation transfers the noodle bar to you and points you toward Banor. Start with the venue setup rather than waiting beside Thaddeus for every later story step.

## Follow Thaddeus Retirement
After his visit to the noodle bar, the chain continues through later messages. It is separate from ordinary venue upgrades.

<details>
<summary>Retirement route spoilers</summary>

1. Read Thaddeus’s message and resolve Agent Teleshko’s document conversation at the noodle bar using its clue.
2. Speak to Banor, then follow later messages about Egress and the ticket.
3. Talk to Huckstash and follow the Metro Hub marker to the Egress gate.
4. Return to Huckstash; after Ava explains the ticket, revisit Egress and speak to Thaddeus.
5. Return when he asks you to. His memory-sharing conversation is followed by a message concluding the quest.

</details>

## Waiting for the next message
The walkthrough’s day count comes from one save, so use the journal and new messages rather than a fixed day. If the log still names Banor or Huckstash, finish that conversation first. If it asks you to return later, keep the current objective instead of restarting the venue introduction.

## Continue the connected story
Keep a save before later Egress conversations. [Ava’s investigation](/characters/ava/) uses related locations but has its own objectives and ending steps.

## First meeting
Ramen Noir, from day 3.

## Plan your romance order
Kenzy and Reena need you to have no partner at the confession and exclude one another on a single save. Finish your choice before completing Deeala, Zaffer, Cale or Mia. Garnet does not count towards this restriction.

This route counts towards the partner restriction. Decide your order before accepting Mia’s apartment invitation. [Compare all seven routes and their order](/guides/romance-ava/)

<h2 id="romance-deeala">Deeala</h2>

**Trigger and preparation：** Reach day 3. Later meetings include Mountain Town, Seaside Boardwalk and Hive Mall; unlock their travel access as you progress. No numerical romance threshold for completing the route is specified.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Deeala](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Ramen Noir**

   **Wait：** From day 3.

   **Dialogue and action：** Respond to her complaint about the meal with a compliment about her appearance; the guide records +1 romance.

   **Condition and branch：** This is the first encounter.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

2. **Near your apartment, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** 2 days after the first meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Hear her complaints about corporate life; the source does not require a particular reply here.

   **Condition and branch：** Complete the Lowtown conversation before looking at the Docks.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

3. **Docks boardwalk**

   **Wait：** 6 hours after the Lowtown meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Talk to her about getting ready for her shift and her eyeliner. No mandatory line is listed.

   **Condition and branch：** Speak to her here before the Mountain Town meeting.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

4. **Mountain Town: rear balcony of the red temple-style restaurant**

   **Wait：** 6 hours after the Docks meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Show interest in learning about the goth scene (+2), then say you would explore it with a suitable companion (+2). If offered, suggest that the relationship could go beyond friendship (+1).

   **Condition and branch：** The extra relationship line depends on how much she likes you; no exact threshold is supplied.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

5. **Seaside Boardwalk**

   **Wait：** 6 hours after Mountain Town.

   **Dialogue and action：** Listen to her landlord problem involving Pilk Dane.

   **Condition and branch：** Finish this conversation to move the meeting to The Stacks.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 5](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

6. **The Stacks**

   **Wait：** 6 hours after Seaside Boardwalk.

   **Dialogue and action：** Say you will consider helping with the roast; this opens Comedy Showdown. A flat rejection ends the route.

   **Condition and branch：** Do not refuse the plan.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 6](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Deeala quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

7. **Follow Deeala’s next conversation / quest marker**

   **Wait：** No additional duration is reported; continue when she asks about your decision.

   **Dialogue and action：** Commit to helping. If you ask about a reward, ask for a date (+1), then agree. Do not keep demanding money or tell her to perform the roast herself. The date reward is the short option “A date?”.

   **Condition and branch：** A demand for cash can be withdrawn in the Steam dialogue record; insisting on it closes the route.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 7](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Deeala quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

8. **Hive Mall**

   **Wait：** After Ava’s coaching; no fixed waiting duration is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** Complete the roast against Pilk Dane. The romance guide reports that the roast outcome does not change the following romantic scene.

   **Condition and branch：** Complete the showdown and return to Deeala. The Steam quest objective describes turning Pilk’s attitude hostile; follow the journal for quest completion.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 8](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Deeala quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

9. **Crow Corner, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** After the showdown; no extra interval is specified.

   **Dialogue and action：** Finish the conversation. The date plan points to Mollyphonic after the LUZ ends; do not wait for that future visit to check the achievement.

   **Condition and branch：** Find Love is recorded at this ending conversation.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 9](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>


**Missable branches：** Rejecting the roast, persisting with the cash demand, or pushing the job back onto Deeala ends the romantic route. Later finding her at Space Port without her apartment is not a restart.

**Completion：** Find Love; a future date is arranged.

## First meeting
Eastern Residential, from its 10-point unlock.

## Plan your romance order
Kenzy and Reena need you to have no partner at the confession and exclude one another on a single save. Finish your choice before completing Deeala, Zaffer, Cale or Mia. Garnet does not count towards this restriction.

Garnet does not count towards the partner restriction and can be scheduled separately. [Compare all seven routes and their order](/guides/romance-ava/)

<small>Basis: [Neoseeker · Dating More Than One Person](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<h2 id="romance-garnet">Garnet</h2>

**Trigger and preparation：** Meet Garnet, then complete Mila’s interview in Metro Hub (20 points). Garnet’s romance does not count as the partner state that blocks Kenzy/Reena.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Garnet](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Eastern Residential**

   **Wait：** Once the district is accessible; no fixed day gate is listed.

   **Dialogue and action：** Talk to Garnet while he is looking for Thaddeus.

   **Condition and branch：** The district needs 10 achievement points.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Garnet · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

2. **Eastern Residential**

   **Wait：** 1 day after meeting him.

   **Dialogue and action：** Sympathise with his unfair experience (+1) and agree to hear his joke (+1).

   **Condition and branch：** Finish the joke conversation.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Garnet · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

3. **Eastern Residential**

   **Wait：** 1 day after the joke conversation.

   **Dialogue and action：** Compliment him despite his tiredness (+1), then offer to help with Mila’s interview without needing a bribe (+1).

   **Condition and branch：** This starts Garnet. Single Dad. Seeks Love.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Garnet · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Garnet quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

4. **Mila’s bench, Metro Hub**

   **Wait：** After accepting the interview; no exact extra wait is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** Answer all three situations positively: tell him about sauce on his chin; pay for dinner and let him pay next time; challenge Janice’s bullying either directly or privately afterwards. The private confrontation receives the larger positive score. The Steam dialogue records +1 for each of the first two positive answers, +1 for a direct challenge and +2 for a private confrontation.

   **Condition and branch：** The community guide asks for at least two favourable answers; take all three recommended answers rather than treating any two as a guaranteed pass. Do not bypass Mila: use her interview result, not general romance, to confirm progress.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Garnet · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Garnet quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

5. **Garnet, Eastern Residential**

   **Wait：** After passing; no extra duration is listed.

   **Dialogue and action：** Tell him you passed and are pleased, then hear his joke.

   **Condition and branch：** Continue to Mila’s final conversation.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Garnet · step 5](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Garnet quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

6. **Mila, Metro Hub**

   **Wait：** When she calls you back; no fixed interval is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** State romantic interest in Garnet, acknowledge that grief cannot be fixed for him, and reassure her that you will not hurt or abandon him.

   **Condition and branch：** This is the romantic continuation, rather than the therapy-only friendship branch.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Garnet · step 6](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Garnet quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

7. **Park trip with Garnet**

   **Wait：** 2 days after the final Mila conversation.

   **Dialogue and action：** Accept the trip to the place connected with Flora’s ashes.

   **Condition and branch：** Find Love completes. Garnet still does not count towards the restrictive partner state.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Garnet · step 7](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>


**Missable branches：** A failed interview sends Mila to other candidates. Skipping her interview or complimenting her to bypass it leads to the alternative grief conversation: paying 800 Lims for therapy makes you a friend, not a romantic partner. Do not treat that payment as a way to buy the romance.

**Completion：** Find Love; no restrictive partner flag.

## The next meeting is missing
Finish the previous conversation, inspect the character list for the current location and check access to the next district. Waits run from the encounter or quest checkpoint named in the step. Reena has no dedicated journal quest. An explicit route-ending answer requires returning to an earlier save.

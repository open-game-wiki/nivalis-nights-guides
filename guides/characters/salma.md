## Understand Find Salma’s Brother
Salma is searching for Seth, her missing brother. Her ramen visits connect to that search. Keep a save before proposing a candidate so you can revisit the conversation if needed.

## Compare the candidates — spoilers

| Candidate | Lead | How to continue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Miso Baron | Mountain Town | Propose the match to Salma and follow her response |
| Idlehands | Central Canyon | This is an alternative theory, not the next required candidate |
| John Doe | A separate theory | Return to Salma for the response if this is your active branch |

The candidate suggestions are branches. Do not work through all three as a mandatory checklist. Read Ava’s current assessment before choosing whom to name.

<details>
<summary>If Salma stops listening — later branch spoilers</summary>

The quest includes a further Miso Baron conversation after Ava identifies him as a likely match and Salma will no longer listen. If that objective appears, speak to Miso instead of repeatedly returning to Salma.

</details>

## Keep the current branch clear
After suggesting a name, read Salma’s reply and the new objective before travelling. When asking for help, include which candidate you proposed and whether the journal names Salma or Miso. See [district planning](/guides/districts/) for organizing the trip.

## Where to start looking
Start your search in the Docks, the area associated with Fauxter in the character reference. This is a search area, not a confirmed first-meeting spot or a guarantee that he is there now. Check his current location in the character list after meeting him. His introduction starts The Songs of Fauxter Glambeat once tutorial step 1 is complete; follow the lyric-writing conversation before the band reunion.

<small>Area: [Fauxter Glambeat — Known area](https://nivalishelp.com/people/fauxter-glambeat). Quest start: [The Songs of Fauxter Glambeat](https://nivalis-nights.com/wiki/quests/the-songs-of-fauxter-glambeat/).</small>

## Start The Songs of Fauxter Glambeat
Speak to Fauxter, help with his lyrics and continue the conversation about the band’s breakup. After his reunion idea appears, follow the member objectives.

## Find the band members

| Role | Character |
| --- | --- |
| DJ | Finn Amano |
| Singer | Andrea Cellini |
| Guitarist — optional | Danny Danetti |
| Drummer | Lois Gyo |
| Theremin player | Olive Nek |

Speak to the person named in the active objective, then return to Fauxter for the reunion stage. Danny’s step is optional and depends on his availability; do not assume it blocks every reunion route.

## Choose a reply that fits the conversation
The members have their own feelings about the breakup. Read their concern before choosing a joke or promise. Keep a save if you want to explore another response.

## If the reunion will not advance
Check whether you are still at lyrical assistance, the breakup conversation or Fauxter’s idea. During recruitment, verify which member objective is unfinished before searching the whole city again. After the member conversations, return to Fauxter rather than waiting at the last musician’s location.

## Start Seafaring at the Docks
Meet Salt Pete when the boat objective appears. Board your boat, take the wheel and discover a new area. Return to Pete, finish the treasure conversation and wait for the contact stage before searching.

## Three treasure checkpoints

| Active stage | Where to look | Next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First treasure | Oil Rig area | Use the boat scanner, then fish for the marked target; return to Pete |
| Second treasure | Waters around Calypso Island | Scan and retrieve the quest object; return to Pete |
| Caleb Foamheart | Mountain Town Boat Hangar | Finish Caleb’s conversation before the third search |
| Third treasure | Fallen ruins | Follow the scanner and current journal target |

Arrival and retrieval are different objectives. Cast into the indicated target, keep the unusual catch and check the updated journal before leaving.

<details>
<summary>Final handoffs — quest spoilers</summary>

After the third treasure, complete the Captain stage with Crabface Jones. Tell Salt Pete the truth, then speak to Caleb Foamheart once more.

</details>

## If a treasure will not appear
Check whether the journal still asks for a return conversation or Pete’s contact. Finish that step before scanning the next region. Keep quest catches until the handoff is resolved. For ordinary catches, open the [fish compendium](/tools/fish-compendium/).

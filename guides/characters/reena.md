## First meeting
Outside Neon Mist, Lowtown, from the start.

## Plan your romance order
Kenzy and Reena need you to have no partner at the confession and exclude one another on a single save. Finish your choice before completing Deeala, Zaffer, Cale or Mia. Garnet does not count towards this restriction.

This route counts towards the partner restriction. Decide your order before accepting Mia’s apartment invitation. [Compare all seven routes and their order](/guides/romance-ava/)

<h2 id="romance-reena">Reena “The Radiance” Coda</h2>

**Trigger and preparation：** Remain without a partner. Finish Noodle Bar Introduction, reach Calypso Island (120 points) and Skyhigh Gardens (90), and have romance ≥3 for the final hotel invitation. This route has no journal quest.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Reena “The Radiance” Coda](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Outside Neon Mist, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** Available from the start; no additional day gate is listed.

   **Dialogue and action：** Introduce yourself.

   **Condition and branch：** The five-day interval starts from this meeting, not an assumed fixed game day.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Reena · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

2. **Inside Neon Mist**

   **Wait：** 5 days after the first meeting, with Noodle Bar Introduction complete.

   **Dialogue and action：** Discuss her light art and compliment her beauty over the display (+1); accept the island meeting.

   **Condition and branch：** Both the elapsed time and introduction completion are needed.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Reena · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

3. **Calypso Island pool**

   **Wait：** 2 days after Neon Mist.

   **Dialogue and action：** Say you came to see her (+2), encourage her ability (+1) and return the compliment (+1). Avoid judging others as immodest (−1). When she asks, confirm you are single.

   **Condition and branch：** Calypso Island needs 120 points. This meeting grants Kompot.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Reena · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

4. **Skyhigh Gardens plaza**

   **Wait：** 2 days after the island meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Say you would travel anywhere to meet her (+1), then accept the nearby hotel-bar suggestion.

   **Condition and branch：** Skyhigh Gardens needs 90 points; the hotel suggestion requires romance ≥3 and no partner. Find Love completes here.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Reena · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>


**Missable branches：** On the last meeting, demanding she make the trip worthwhile costs 2; immediately explain it as a failed flirt to apologise. Defensively denying mouldy food or walking away to your shop resets romance to 0; the latter also triggers a bad news write-up. Naming Rights offers an optional +1 for calling its situation romantic.

**Completion：** Kompot and Find Love; counts as a partner.

## Start with Ava and the noodle bar
Ava introduces herself in your Lowtown apartment as Thaddeus’s former companion. Follow the cab to Meridian Market, speak to Thaddeus, then ask Banor about the noodle bar. Use the [first-session checklist](/guides/first-hour-guide/) for equipment and stock.

## Match your investigation to the journal
Where Is Ava? follows her past. These are checkpoints, not a requirement to repeat every conversation:

| Journal topic | Contact or action |
| --- | --- |
| Gan’s human past | Gan Coilson and Ceres Coilson |
| Old Network or books | Banor, Huckstash and Gunger Hahn |
| Pain or cryogenics | Dr Fred, R M Alken and Alan Fontus |
| Later research | Hepzibah Gimble and Nabla |

Follow the named contact for your active objective. Some investigation topics and contacts are alternative branches. When the journal says Ava is researching, finish the current conversation before looking for a later location.

<details>
<summary>Late investigation and ending spoilers</summary>

Later objectives involve Rosetta, the old TheraPay bot and Rosco Foreman. The Old Network terminal requires a password clue from Gan or Ceres. The cryo-chamber objective leads to Egress; birthday conversations provide another door clue. Keep a separate save before the final chamber and Ava’s decision.

</details>

## If the terminal is still locked
Distinguish the terminal access clue from the later Egress room clue. Revisit the contact named in the journal instead of trying the same code at both doors. For the parallel storyline, see [Thaddeus’s retirement](/characters/thaddeus/).

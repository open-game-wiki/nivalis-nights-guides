## Hospitality Workforce & Operations Overview

Running a hospitality business in **Nivalis** quickly outgrows solo labor once you manage more than a single counter. With strict daily curfew sirens at 2:00 AM, time spent chopping scallions or restocking dry noodles is time taken away from exploring the city, fishing canal reefs, or courting contacts. Managing hired staff and tailoring independent menus per venue is the key to operating multiple thriving locations.

---

## 1. Staff Roles & The Starter Workforce

In official gameplay showcases, developer ION LANDS confirms that players must actively hire and manage employees across three distinct operational branches:

### Starter Kitchen Assistant (Banor)
- When taking over **Ramen Noir** in Meridian Market from Thaddeus Carmunis, Banor is already on site assisting with food preparation. This gives solo players a baseline support system during opening trading hours.

### Line Cooks & Prep Workers
- As you acquire additional noodle bars or full-scale diners, hiring extra line cooks ensures orders are prepared promptly during the intense **11:30 Lunch Rush** and **18:00 Dinner Rush**.

### Front-of-House Service Staff
- Table service, greeting arriving patrons, and taking payment become essential when scaling from walk-up counter carts to brick-and-mortar dining rooms with 10+ covers.

## 2. Independent Per-Venue Menu Management

Rather than maintaining a universal menu applied automatically across all storefronts, each venue possesses its own independent menu roster. This allows smart operators to align food options with district demographics:

| Venue Tier / District | Recommended Menu Strategy | Core Items | Average Ticket Margin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transit Menus (Metro Hub)** | Fast turnover, grab-and-go convenience | Bao buns, shoyu broth cups, hot VegCoffee | 60% margin, high volume |
| **Commercial Plazas (Central Canyon)** | Multi-course fine dining, executive lunches | Synth-steaks, grilled canal catches, wine | High ticket size |
| **Nightlife Lounges (Upper Spires)** | High-margin evening drinks & artisanal snacks | Craft cocktails, neon liqueurs, fried snacks | 80%+ profit margin |

## 3. The Venue Level 3 Manager Role

Reaching **Venue Level 3** unlocks the ability to recruit a dedicated **Store Manager**. Managers transform a hands-on kitchen into a fully automated passive enterprise:

1. **Autonomous Cross-Venue Restocking**:
   - Running out of ingredients mid-rush causes negative customer reviews. Managers coordinate bulk replenishment across all connected venues without requiring manual cart trips.
2. **Greenhouse Harvest Logistics**:
   - Produce grown in your rented greenhouses (scallions, mushrooms, and printed proteins) is automatically collected and routed to walk-in restaurant larders.
3. **Staff Morale & Speed Buffs**:
   - An on-site manager increases kitchen throughput and employee service speed by up to 25%, ensuring orders reach tables before customer patience expires.

## 4. The 10-Level Task Priority Pipeline (Update #2)

Under Update #2, employee AI follows a prioritized 10-level workflow logic:
- **Priority 1–3**: Clearing dirty tables, greeting seated guests, taking active orders.
- **Priority 4–6**: Firing orders on cooking stations, plating finished dishes, expediting to floor staff.
- **Priority 7–10**: Restocking counter bins, wiping surfaces, and checking chiller temperature logs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you run Ramen Noir without hiring additional staff?
Yes, for your first week, you and your default assistant Banor can handle the lunch and dinner rush at Ramen Noir without taking on extra payroll expenses.

### How do you unlock the Venue Manager role?
You must upgrade your venue to **Venue Level 3** through sustained 4-star customer ratings and meeting the cumulative revenue milestone for that storefront.

### Will staff continue working if I am away fishing or exploring?
Yes! Once hired and assigned to active shifts, staff will continue preparing food and serving guests during open hours according to their designated shift schedule.

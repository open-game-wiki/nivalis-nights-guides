## Follow the current conversation
Read the active quest and finish the current conversation before travelling to the next objective. Check incoming messages when the journal does not give you a new destination.

## Keep track of your choices
Keep the quest title, dialogue choice and resulting objective together. Save before important branches so you can return to a decision you want to revisit.

## Continue into the late story
For the later Where Is Ava? checkpoints, see [Tier 4 venues and late quests](/guides/tier-4-and-late-quests/). That section contains ending spoilers; keep a separate save before following it.

## Choose a route and plan the order
Jump to a character, then expand the route for each meeting's location, wait and dialogue choices. Timings follow the in-game sequence and run from the named previous encounter or quest checkpoint, rather than real-world elapsed time.

Kenzy and Reena need you to have no partner at the confession, and exclude one another on a single save. Finish your choice before completing Deeala, Zaffer, Cale or Mia. Garnet does not count towards that restriction. Decide this order before accepting Mia's apartment invitation.

Dialogue directions describe the meaning of a choice, with a few short original cues. Chinese is a lookup translation, not official game localization. An unreported delay is labelled unreported, rather than assumed to be zero. Every step links to its character section or quest record.

| [Deeala](#romance-deeala) | Ramen Noir, from day 3. | Yes |

<h2 id="romance-deeala">Deeala</h2>

[Open Deeala character guide](/characters/deeala/)

**Trigger and preparation：** Reach day 3. Later meetings include Mountain Town, Seaside Boardwalk and Hive Mall; unlock their travel access as you progress. No numerical romance threshold for completing the route is specified.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Deeala](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Ramen Noir**

   **Wait：** From day 3.

   **Dialogue and action：** Respond to her complaint about the meal with a compliment about her appearance; the guide records +1 romance.

   **Condition and branch：** This is the first encounter.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

2. **Near your apartment, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** 2 days after the first meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Hear her complaints about corporate life; the source does not require a particular reply here.

   **Condition and branch：** Complete the Lowtown conversation before looking at the Docks.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

3. **Docks boardwalk**

   **Wait：** 6 hours after the Lowtown meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Talk to her about getting ready for her shift and her eyeliner. No mandatory line is listed.

   **Condition and branch：** Speak to her here before the Mountain Town meeting.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

4. **Mountain Town: rear balcony of the red temple-style restaurant**

   **Wait：** 6 hours after the Docks meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Show interest in learning about the goth scene (+2), then say you would explore it with a suitable companion (+2). If offered, suggest that the relationship could go beyond friendship (+1).

   **Condition and branch：** The extra relationship line depends on how much she likes you; no exact threshold is supplied.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

5. **Seaside Boardwalk**

   **Wait：** 6 hours after Mountain Town.

   **Dialogue and action：** Listen to her landlord problem involving Pilk Dane.

   **Condition and branch：** Finish this conversation to move the meeting to The Stacks.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 5](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

6. **The Stacks**

   **Wait：** 6 hours after Seaside Boardwalk.

   **Dialogue and action：** Say you will consider helping with the roast; this opens Comedy Showdown. A flat rejection ends the route.

   **Condition and branch：** Do not refuse the plan.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 6](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Deeala quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

7. **Follow Deeala’s next conversation / quest marker**

   **Wait：** No additional duration is reported; continue when she asks about your decision.

   **Dialogue and action：** Commit to helping. If you ask about a reward, ask for a date (+1), then agree. Do not keep demanding money or tell her to perform the roast herself. The date reward is the short option “A date?”.

   **Condition and branch：** A demand for cash can be withdrawn in the Steam dialogue record; insisting on it closes the route.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 7](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Deeala quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

8. **Hive Mall**

   **Wait：** After Ava’s coaching; no fixed waiting duration is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** Complete the roast against Pilk Dane. The romance guide reports that the roast outcome does not change the following romantic scene.

   **Condition and branch：** Complete the showdown and return to Deeala. The Steam quest objective describes turning Pilk’s attitude hostile; follow the journal for quest completion.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 8](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Deeala quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

9. **Crow Corner, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** After the showdown; no extra interval is specified.

   **Dialogue and action：** Finish the conversation. The date plan points to Mollyphonic after the LUZ ends; do not wait for that future visit to check the achievement.

   **Condition and branch：** Find Love is recorded at this ending conversation.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Deeala · step 9](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>


**Missable branches：** Rejecting the roast, persisting with the cash demand, or pushing the job back onto Deeala ends the romantic route. Later finding her at Space Port without her apartment is not a restart.

**Completion：** Find Love; a future date is arranged.

</details>

<h2 id="romance-zaffer">Zaffer</h2>

[Open Zaffer character guide](/characters/zaffer/)

**Trigger and preparation：** Meet Zaffer and avoid strongly corporate choices. The route needs Neon Tubes, Mountain Town access and Delbert Cherry’s Oil Rig Separatists quest. The favour requires romance ≥4; the apartment visit requires ≥5.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Zaffer](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Graffiti Alley, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** First meeting; no day gate is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** Take the anti-corporate side when discussing the corps.

   **Condition and branch：** Corporate alignment matters later; this is not a generic friendship grind.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

2. **Side Bar, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** 2 days after the first meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Show interest in his sign-hacking offer and invite him to visit; the invitation reply gives +1 romance.

   **Condition and branch：** Finish the Side Bar encounter.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

3. **Ramen Noir**

   **Wait：** 2 days after the Side Bar meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Respond positively to the flirting (+2), then accept the sign project to begin Neon for Zaffer.

   **Condition and branch：** Refusing the sign stops his story. Flirting is conditioned on not siding with the corps.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

4. **Mertie’s neon stall, Meridian Market; fallback: Hive Mall hardware vendor**

   **Wait：** After accepting the neon job; no timer is reported.

   **Dialogue and action：** Ask Aunt Mertie for Neon Tubes using Zaffer’s connection. If she is unavailable after the murders, buy the tubes in Hive Mall.

   **Condition and branch：** Carry Neon Tubes back to Zaffer.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Zaffer quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

5. **Graffiti Alley, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** After obtaining the tubes.

   **Dialogue and action：** Confirm delivery, affirm the friendship, then leave room for a romantic relationship; reassure him and agree to the plan. The recommended sequence totals +2 romance here. Key short replies are “Maybe...”, “Never.” and “I will.” in that order.

   **Condition and branch：** Do not define the relationship as only friendship or deny that you are friends; those replies skip the date.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 5](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Zaffer quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

6. **Mountain Town, Byal Lapin graffiti**

   **Wait：** Follow the date objective; no extra delay is specified.

   **Dialogue and action：** Play along with his rebellious image (+1) and express that you are glad he brought you (+1).

   **Condition and branch：** Finish the date, but keep the Delbert favour on your checklist.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 6](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

7. **Ramen Noir**

   **Wait：** 1 day after the date.

   **Dialogue and action：** Accept the Neon Sign he brings.

   **Condition and branch：** The gift alone is not the apartment-ending trigger.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 7](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

8. **Oil Rig / Lowtown side quest**

   **Wait：** Advance the Delbert favour below; its opening wait is 1 day.

   **Dialogue and action：** Include Zaffer in Delbert’s plan, then accept Zaffer’s offer to visit your apartment that night.

   **Condition and branch：** At least 4 romance points when asking for the favour; avoid strong corporate alignment.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 8](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

9. **Your apartment**

   **Wait：** After both the sign gift and Delbert favour are complete; no precise arrival hour is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** Give the reassuring response that you would not abandon him.

   **Condition and branch：** At least 5 romance points before the visit; Find Love is recorded here.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer · step 9](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>


**Required Oil Rig Separatists connection**

1. **Delbert Cherry, Oil Rig**

   **Wait：** Start before you need the final favour.

   **Dialogue and action：** Hear his plan rather than condemning it as dangerous.

   **Condition and branch：** The hostile corporate response makes Delbert refuse to work with you.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer / Oil Rig Separatists · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

2. **Oil Rig**

   **Wait：** The next day after meeting Delbert.

   **Dialogue and action：** Accept the recruitment request in Oil Rig Separatists.

   **Condition and branch：** Speak to Botan Noss, Reena, Sanya Predex and Finlay Basin; at least one must join.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer / Oil Rig Separatists · step 2–3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

3. **Return to Delbert**

   **Wait：** After recruiting; no extra timer is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** When publicity comes up, offer the contact who works with neon signs, even if Reena joined.

   **Condition and branch：** Using the Reena alternative leaves Zaffer out of this connection.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer / Oil Rig Separatists · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

4. **Zaffer, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** Ask after his date invitation, once romance is at least 4.

   **Dialogue and action：** Ask for the neon favour and say he can bring it to your apartment that night.

   **Condition and branch：** Then complete the sign-gift and romance ≥5 requirements for the ending visit.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Zaffer / Oil Rig Separatists · step 5](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>


**Missable branches：** The Mountain Town date is not enough. Choosing friendship, refusing the neon job, excluding Zaffer from Delbert’s publicity plan, or failing to recruit anyone for Delbert can block the route.

**Completion：** Neon Sign and Find Love.

</details>

<h2 id="romance-cale-wyer">Cale Wyer</h2>

[Open Cale Wyer character guide](/characters/cale-wyer/)

**Trigger and preparation：** Reach day 6 and progress the main story. The date needs Skyhigh Gardens (90 achievement points), Calypso Island (120) and a Reservation Chip.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Cale Wyer](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Ramen Noir balcony**

   **Wait：** From day 6.

   **Dialogue and action：** Respond appreciatively to his compliment (+1 romance).

   **Condition and branch：** Day 6 / main-story gate is also listed by the Steam quest guide.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Cale Wyer · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Cale Wyer quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

2. **Lowtown taxi rank**

   **Wait：** 1 day after the first meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** During Calling Cale Wyer, offer him a meal at your stand without charging him (+1).

   **Condition and branch：** Complete the free-meal invitation.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Cale Wyer · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Cale Wyer quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

3. **Ramen Noir**

   **Wait：** 1 day after the Lowtown conversation.

   **Dialogue and action：** Confirm you genuinely wanted to offer the meal, frame it as a date and say you want to see him again; the recommended sequence gives +3. Do not replace the meal with a 10% discount (−1).

   **Condition and branch：** Finish the meal conversation.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Cale Wyer · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Cale Wyer quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

4. **Ava’s message / Skyhigh Gardens invitation**

   **Wait：** The next day.

   **Dialogue and action：** Accept Cale’s date invitation. Saying you changed your mind permanently ends Calling Cale Wyer. The acceptance is “Lead the way.”

   **Condition and branch：** This is the route-ending refusal to avoid.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Cale Wyer · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

5. **Skyhigh Gardens overlook**

   **Wait：** After accepting; no additional duration is given.

   **Dialogue and action：** Hear about his childhood restaurant on Calypso Island.

   **Condition and branch：** Unlock Skyhigh Gardens with 90 achievement points.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Cale Wyer · step 5](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

6. **Calypso Island pool house**

   **Wait：** After the overlook meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Book a table. Finn supplies the Reservation Chip if The Lost Art of Anthony G is complete; otherwise Sage Hullen supplies it.

   **Condition and branch：** Calypso Island requires 120 achievement points. Do not assume Finn is the only source.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Cale Wyer · step 6](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

7. **Return to the Skyhigh Gardens overlook**

   **Wait：** After obtaining the Reservation Chip.

   **Dialogue and action：** Deliver the chip to Cale and confirm dinner.

   **Condition and branch：** This conversation teaches Trout with Garlic Lemon Butter Herb Sauce.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Cale Wyer · step 7](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Cale Wyer quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

8. **Calypso Island dinner**

   **Wait：** 2 days after giving Cale the chip.

   **Dialogue and action：** Attend dinner and finish the toast. The guide reports that dinner dialogue does not change the romance completion.

   **Condition and branch：** Find Love completes at dinner, rather than at the earlier overlook.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Cale Wyer · step 8](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>


**Missable branches：** Do not decline the invitation played by Ava. If Finn is absent, check Sage for the reservation chip. Having a partner does not have the Kenzy/Reena restriction here.

**Completion：** Trout recipe and Find Love.

</details>

<h2 id="romance-reena">Reena “The Radiance” Coda</h2>

[Open Reena “The Radiance” Coda character guide](/characters/reena/)

**Trigger and preparation：** Remain without a partner. Finish Noodle Bar Introduction, reach Calypso Island (120 points) and Skyhigh Gardens (90), and have romance ≥3 for the final hotel invitation. This route has no journal quest.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Reena “The Radiance” Coda](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Outside Neon Mist, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** Available from the start; no additional day gate is listed.

   **Dialogue and action：** Introduce yourself.

   **Condition and branch：** The five-day interval starts from this meeting, not an assumed fixed game day.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Reena · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

2. **Inside Neon Mist**

   **Wait：** 5 days after the first meeting, with Noodle Bar Introduction complete.

   **Dialogue and action：** Discuss her light art and compliment her beauty over the display (+1); accept the island meeting.

   **Condition and branch：** Both the elapsed time and introduction completion are needed.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Reena · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

3. **Calypso Island pool**

   **Wait：** 2 days after Neon Mist.

   **Dialogue and action：** Say you came to see her (+2), encourage her ability (+1) and return the compliment (+1). Avoid judging others as immodest (−1). When she asks, confirm you are single.

   **Condition and branch：** Calypso Island needs 120 points. This meeting grants Kompot.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Reena · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

4. **Skyhigh Gardens plaza**

   **Wait：** 2 days after the island meeting.

   **Dialogue and action：** Say you would travel anywhere to meet her (+1), then accept the nearby hotel-bar suggestion.

   **Condition and branch：** Skyhigh Gardens needs 90 points; the hotel suggestion requires romance ≥3 and no partner. Find Love completes here.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Reena · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>


**Missable branches：** On the last meeting, demanding she make the trip worthwhile costs 2; immediately explain it as a failed flirt to apologise. Defensively denying mouldy food or walking away to your shop resets romance to 0; the latter also triggers a bad news write-up. Naming Rights offers an optional +1 for calling its situation romantic.

**Completion：** Kompot and Find Love; counts as a partner.

</details>

<h2 id="romance-kenzy-voan">Kenzy Voan</h2>

[Open Kenzy Voan character guide](/characters/kenzy-voan/)

**Trigger and preparation：** Finish Noodle Bar Introduction, remain single for the final confession, and reach Seaside Boardwalk (70 points). The romance reward dialogue requires at least 3 romance points.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Kenzy Voan](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

1. **Central Canyon, near Side Bar**

   **Wait：** From day 6, after the introduction.

   **Dialogue and action：** Offer to help restore her reputation without asking for payment (+1), starting The Club Rat Kids. Walking away ends the story.

   **Condition and branch：** Steam lists after day 5 and introduction completion; this is day 6 onward.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Kenzy Voan · step 1](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Kenzy Voan quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

2. **Finn Amano, Metro Hub**

   **Wait：** After accepting; no extra duration is supplied.

   **Dialogue and action：** Ask Finn to return to the crew.

   **Condition and branch：** Return to Kenzy afterwards.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Kenzy Voan · step 2](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Kenzy Voan quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

3. **Kenzy’s reputation conversation**

   **Wait：** After Finn; no extra interval is given.

   **Dialogue and action：** Agree to help and take five Holo Flyers. Do not reject the job twice.

   **Condition and branch：** Two refusals fail the quest.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Kenzy Voan · step 3](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Kenzy Voan quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

4. **Five district bulletin boards**

   **Wait：** During the flyer task; no waiting timer is listed.

   **Dialogue and action：** Post one flyer in each of Meridian Market, Docks, Central Canyon, Metro Hub and Seaside Boardwalk. Optional history talk: prioritise talking to Kenzy (+1).

   **Condition and branch：** All five boards are required. Seaside Boardwalk needs 70 points.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Kenzy Voan · step 4](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Kenzy Voan quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

5. **Return to Kenzy**

   **Wait：** After all five flyers.

   **Dialogue and action：** Offer to take her wherever she wants and say you want to dance with her (+2 together); saying the work was for her is another recorded +1 option.

   **Condition and branch：** Learn Salted Taimen and follow her lead to Zaffer.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Kenzy Voan · step 5](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Kenzy Voan quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

6. **Graffiti Alley → Eastern Residential, near Fusion Point**

   **Wait：** 1 day after asking Zaffer to make the graffiti.

   **Dialogue and action：** Ask for a Club Rat Kids mural, then find and photograph the finished graffiti.

   **Condition and branch：** The photograph is an explicit Steam objective; simply seeing the mural is not enough.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Kenzy Voan · step 6](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Kenzy Voan quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

7. **Show Kenzy the graffiti result**

   **Wait：** After taking the photograph.

   **Dialogue and action：** Choose to do it for her rather than insist on payment, then say you want to remain with her. The two key choices are “Anything for you.”, then “I want to stay around.”

   **Condition and branch：** The romantic reward reply needs romance ≥3. This path gives up the 1,500 Lims payment; taking the money ends as friends.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Kenzy Voan · step 7](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Kenzy Voan quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

8. **Neon Mist dance floor, Lowtown**

   **Wait：** 15 minutes after the romantic reward conversation.

   **Dialogue and action：** Make an explicit declaration of love to Kenzy rather than merely congratulate her. The confession is “I love you, Kenzy.”

   **Condition and branch：** The confession appears only with no existing partner; it completes Find Love.

   <small>Sources：[Neoseeker Romance · Kenzy Voan · step 8](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance) · [Tee · Steam Complete Quest Guide · Kenzy Voan quest objectives / dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522)</small>

<h2 id="romance-mia-jay">Mia Jay</h2>

[Open Mia Jay character guide](/characters/mia-jay/)

**Trigger and preparation：** Unlock the Sewers with 30 achievement points, collect any seven of thirteen quest k20 Tumblers, and reach romance ≥2 before the apartment invitation.

<small>Basis：[Neoseeker · Mia Jay](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance)</small>

<details>
<summary>Full route and story branches</summary>

## How does Ava differ from these seven routes?
Ava has an emotional response near the main-story ending, rather than one of the seven date routes. Advance the Old Network and body investigation in Where is Ava? instead of repeating generic conversations.

<details><summary>Ava's ending — major story spoilers</summary>

Late objectives include curfew visits; hiring Nabla for the investigation asks for 1,000 Lims. See the [late-story guide](/guides/tier-4-and-late-quests/) for connecting checkpoints. The final goodbye includes answers expressing hope of meeting again that raise romance, alongside alternative goodbyes. Keep a separate save before the terminal and cryo-chamber sequence. No fixed romance value is presented as guaranteeing an ending.

Basis: [Tee, Where is Ava? objectives and dialogue](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819145522); [Neoseeker, Characters You Can Only Flirt With](https://www.neoseeker.com/nivalis-nights/Romance).

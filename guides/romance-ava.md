## 1. Key Characters & Romance System Overview

In Nivalis, social relationships are not limited to shallow binary romance options. Your daily conversations, choice of dinner venues, shared leisure activities, and business interactions determine how citizens perceive you.

The game features four distinct long-term relationship outcomes:
1. **Close Friend**: Provides unique social perks, discounts at local shops, and casual hangout options.
2. **Business Partner**: Unlocks co-owned hospitality ventures, shared logistics chains, and joint revenue dividends.
3. **Professional Rival**: Triggers competition in district culinary ratings and dynamic price wars.
4. **"Something More" (Romance)**: Unlocks intimate story cutscenes, cohabiting in your apartment, and exclusive character endings.

## 2. Ava's Complete Storyline & Meeting Milestones

Ava is a fiercely independent freelance courier and mechanic who spends her days fixing HOVA vehicles near the **Midtown Sky-Docks** and her evenings browsing music stalls in the Lower Canals.

### Progression Timeline
* **Act 1: The Stalled Engine**: Encounter Ava during Chapter 1 while retrieving starter kitchen supplies. Assist her with a diagnostic tool to unlock her contact number in your personal comms datapad.
* **Act 2: Canal Coffee Breaks**: Visit her garage between 14:00 and 17:00 on weekdays. Bringing her a fresh cup of **Cyber-Mocha** boosts her trust gauge significantly.
* **Act 3: The Courier Debt Dilemma**: Ava's syndicate employer demands an unreasonable contract penalty. You can either loan her **5,000 Credits** or help her hack the logistics mainframe using Salma's decryptor keys.
* **Act 4: The Rooftop Confession**: After reaching Social Rank 7, invite Ava to the Upper Skyway Observation Deck on a clear evening. Choosing the dialogue option *"I built a home here, but it's only real with you"* locks in the romance path.

## 4. The 01:30 Curfew Safety Protocol

Nivalis enforces a mandatory civilian curfew at **02:00 sharp**. Police surveillance drones actively sweep pedestrian bridges and canal docks with heavy spotlights.

### Why Dates Go Wrong After Midnight
* If your date runs past **01:30**, Ava will express anxiety about siren alerts.
* Failing to escort her back to her apartment lobby before **02:00** triggers a drone intercept encounter. You will receive a **1,500 Credit municipal violation fine**, and Ava's affinity drops by **15 points** due to safety negligence.
* **Pro-Tip**: Park your motorboat within 50 meters of the date location so you can make a high-speed waterway escape if time runs short.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I date multiple characters simultaneously?
Nivalis allows you to build high friendship ranks with multiple characters, but entering a declared romantic partnership with Ava will lock out romantic epilogues with secondary candidates.

### What if I miss Ava's scheduled hangout?
If you arrive more than 1 in-game hour late to an agreed hangout, Ava will send a disappointed comms ping. You will need to bring her a gift the following day to restore scheduled hangouts.

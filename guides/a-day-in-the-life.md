## 1. Two Playstyles: Tycoon Rush vs. Slow Cyber Life

In **Nivalis Nights**, you dictate the cadence of your daily life:

- **The Corporate Tycoon**: Relentlessly manages multiple restaurant menus, tracks wholesale commodity price shifts at 06:00, optimizes staff shifts, and squeezes every credit out of the 18:00 dinner rush before depositing profits.
- **The Slow Atmospheric Sim**: Sips morning VegCoffee while watching holographic rain stream down apartment windows, cruises the canals aboard a motorboat, chats with companion Ava at the docks, and plays casual chess matches on Calypso Island.

## 3. Balancing Stamina & Burnout

Unlike basic RPGs where sleep is optional, *Nivalis Nights* links character physical endurance directly to your operations:
- **Low Stamina Penalty**: Running on empty slows movement speed, degrades cooking precision (causing burnt dishes), and triggers premature blackouts.
- **Stamina Boosters**: Fresh morning VegCoffee, herbal canal teas, and dining on your own high-tier restaurant meals provide sustained stamina buffers during overtime shifts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I skip days or sleep early?
Yes, interacting with your bed allows you to sleep at any time to advance the clock to the following morning at 06:00.

### What happens if I forget to open my restaurant during lunch?
Your venue simply remains closed for that shift. You won't earn revenue, but you also won't receive negative customer reviews since no customers were served.

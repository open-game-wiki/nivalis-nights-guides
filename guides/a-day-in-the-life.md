## Choose three priorities
Pick one venue task, one place to explore and one conversation to follow. Check the quest log before leaving so you can combine errands along the way.

## Prepare for the trip
Check supplies, the route and any purchases you need. Leave room in your budget for the next operating period before spending on decoration or expansion.

## Leave a next step
At the end of the session, note the next action and any missing item. Return to that objective next time instead of retracing the whole day.

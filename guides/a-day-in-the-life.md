## A flexible day, not a mandatory timetable
The official September 4 preview presents daily life as a choice of pace. It is not a published schedule that every player must follow.

## An optional planning template
Pick one business task, one exploration objective and one conversation you want to pursue. Check the current quest requirements before travelling. This is our way of organizing a session, not a statement about NPC availability.

## Leave room for change
If an objective needs an item you do not have, record it and decide whether to source it now or later. At the end of a session, note the next action rather than relying on an assumed universal Day 3 or Day 7 unlock.

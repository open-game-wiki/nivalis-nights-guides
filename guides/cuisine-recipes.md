## Culinary System & Restaurant Economy Overview

In the neon rain of Nivalis, food is survival, status, and high-margin profit. From humble late-night street comfort food like **Crispy Cola Chicken** to social party centerpieces like **Meridian Pizza** and delicate seasonal specialties, mastering the city's 190+ recipes across 9 official menu categories forms the core of your hospitality empire.

---

## 6 Iconic Signature Dishes

In developer updates and the official *Tourist Map* brochure, six core signature dishes define the culinary landscape of Nivalis:

### 1. Ramen Noir (Signature Dish)
- **Origin**: The legacy bowl inherited from Thaddeus Carmunis at your starter stall in Meridian Market.
- **Ingredients**: Hand-Pulled Noodles (1x) + Dark Broth Base (1x) + Breaded Cutlet (1x) + Scallions/Egg (1x)
- **Role**: High-margin daily workhorse for lunchtime crowds.

### 2. Crispy Cola Chicken (Street Comfort Snack)
- **Origin**: Synthesized chicken thighs braised in a reduced spiced cyber-cola glaze, battered and flash-fried.
- **Ingredients**: Synth-Chicken (1x) + Bottled Cola (1x) + Garlic Ginger Paste (1x)
- **Role**: Inexpensive crowd-pleaser enjoyed by cinema goers and late-night programmers.

### 3. Summer Garden Salad (Strictly Seasonal)
- **Origin**: Crisp hydroponic greens tossed with canal-side radishes and citrus vinaigrette.
- **Ingredients**: Hydro-Lettuce (2x) + Cherry Radish (1x) + Synth-Vinaigrette (1x)
- **CRITICAL LAW**: **Strictly banned in Autumn & Winter** under municipal quarantine edicts. Serving this dish out-of-season triggers severe public health fines!

### 4. Meridian Pizza (Group Dining Centerpiece)
- **Origin**: Hand-stretched dough topped with synthesized mozzarella, canal herbs, and spicy pepperoni substitute.
- **Ingredients**: Refined Dough (2x) + Synth-Cheese (2x) + Spiced Pepperoni (1x)
- **Role**: Best for high table-ticket sizes and group seating arrangements.

### 5. VegCoffee (Morning Stamina Staple)
- **Origin**: Roast root grains brewed with purified water and condensed milk.
- **Ingredients**: Roast Root Grains (2x) + Purified Water (1x) + Condensed Milk (1x)
- **Role**: Essential morning pickup item that provides an immediate stamina restoration buff.

### 6. Glowing Salmon Nigiri (High-Roller Delicacy)
- **Origin**: Bioluminescent salmon fresh-caught from lower canal reefs, draped over vinegared rice with hydro-wasabi.
- **Ingredients**: Glowing Salmon (1x) + Seasoned Rice (1x) + Fresh Hydro-Wasabi (1x)
- **Role**: Premium luxury dish with near 100% profit margins when caught directly from your personal boat.

## Patch 1.0 & Update #2: Freshness & Storage Mechanics

With recent game updates, raw ingredients feature realistic decay systems:

1. **FIFO Inventory Flow**: The kitchen automatically prioritizes consuming older ingredients first.
2. **Refrigeration Grades**: Basic cupboards allow meat and seafood to spoil within 24 in-game hours. Upgrading to a **Commercial Chiller Unit** extends shelf life to 5 in-game days.
3. **Freshness Review Metric**: Serving expired or degrading ingredients inflicts an immediate penalty on your venue's *"Food Freshness"* review rating, driving away discerning diners.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you serve Summer Garden Salad all year round?
No! Summer Garden Salad is strictly prohibited during autumn and winter seasons by municipal quarantine laws. Serving it during restricted seasons incurs hefty city health fines.

### How do you get 100% profit margin on seafood dishes?
By catching the raw fish yourself (such as Glowing Salmon) using your motorboat in the canal waterways rather than purchasing them from retail market vendors.

### What equipment is required to cook Ramen Noir?
You need a basic Broth Boiling Station, a Noodle Prep Counter, and a Deep Fryer for the crispy cutlet topper.

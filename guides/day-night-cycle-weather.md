## Check the city clock
Check the in-game time and current objective before travelling. Combine nearby errands so you can spend more of the session on the activity you came for.

## Keep time units clear
When planning a trip or a harvest cycle, distinguish real minutes from in-game hours. Note the visible clock before and after an activity to estimate your next visit.

## Compare similar outings
Keep the location and equipment similar when comparing fishing trips in different weather. For venue budgets, use recorded sales and costs in the [profit planner](/tools/business-calculator/).

## Weather is part of the city
The official store describes a weather system and curfew-related activity. It does not provide a fixed list of weather states, their probabilities, or a table of weather-dependent business bonuses.

## Keep time units explicit
When taking notes, distinguish real minutes from in-game time. Record the visible clock before and after an activity. Do not transfer a timing estimate from a different build without checking it.

## Test a weather claim
Compare the same location and activity across several conditions. A busier evening is not enough to isolate rain as the cause. Keep financial planning based on observed sales rather than an assumed weather multiplier.

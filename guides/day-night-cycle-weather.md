## Urban Rhythm & Environmental Dynamics Overview

In the vertical metropolis of **Nivalis**, time and weather are active economic variables rather than cosmetic backdrops. Shifting cloud cover, torrential acid downpours, and nocturnal curfew klaxons dictate consumer spending habits, pedestrian travel paths, and restaurant utility overhead.

---

## The 5 Dynamic Weather Patterns & Commercial Impact

| Weather Pattern | Frequency | Street Impact | Optimal Restaurant / Business Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Neon Drizzle** | High (Default) | Standard pedestrian foot traffic | Balanced daily menu; steady counter sales |
| **Acid Downpour** | Medium | Outdoor plazas empty; shoppers seek shelter | **Surge in indoor noodle bars**; hot soups & ramen see +40% demand |
| **Volumetric Smog** | Medium | Slows watercraft navigation; chilling air | High sales for hot VegCoffee and ginger broths |
| **Severe Thunderstorm** | Low | High canal chop; boat travel hazardous | Nightclub & speakeasy indoor alcohol sales jump +60% |
| **Clear Neon Sky** | Very Rare | High pedestrian mobility across sky bridges | Open-air dining and outdoor cocktail lounges thrive |

## 1. Commercial Rush Hours Across the Day

Customer patronage concentrates around two primary daily windows:

### The Lunch Rush (11:30 – 14:00)
- **Customer Demographics**: Local office workers, factory laborers, and transit commuters.
- **Spending Behavior**: Fast turnover, single-bowl meals, grab-and-go sides.
- **Top Dishes**: Chicken Noodle Soup, Shoyu Ramen, fried dumplings.

### The Evening Dinner Rush (18:00 – 21:30)
- **Customer Demographics**: Couples, corporate executives, dining groups.
- **Spending Behavior**: Multi-course meals, appetizers, alcoholic beverages, high tip percentages.
- **Top Dishes**: Tonkotsu Ramen, Meridian Pizza, Glowing Salmon Nigiri.

## 2. Fluctuating Energy & Utility Overhead

Operating commercial kitchens and hydroponic greenhouses incurs electricity bills:
- **Peak Hour Tariffs**: Between 17:00 and 22:00, the municipal grid charges higher kWh utility rates for kitchen appliances and neon signage.
- **Greenhouse Climate Control**: Maintaining heaters during cold smog days increases energy consumption. Automating heating timers saves up to 20% on monthly overhead.

---

## 3. The 02:00 AM Martial Curfew Enforcement

- **Sirens at 02:00 AM**: All legal street commerce halts, and corporate security drones sweep public catwalks.
- **Penalties**: Getting caught outside results in stamina collapse, an immediate **1,200 Credits medical fine**, and potential confiscation of backpack goods.
- **Safe Zones**: Sleeping inside your apartment bed or anchoring your motorboat in canal waters completely negates curfew fines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does acid rain damage your character?
Acid rain drains your personal stamina slightly faster if you spend long periods outdoors without an umbrella or weather-resistant clothing, but it will not kill you instantly.

### Do customers leave restaurants when the rain starts?
No! When rain intensifies, pedestrians outside actively flee into nearby open diners and noodle bars, creating an unexpected sales surge for indoor venues.

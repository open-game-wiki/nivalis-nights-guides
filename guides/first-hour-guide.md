## First 60 Minutes Survival Overview

Your first sixty minutes in **Nivalis** establish the foundational rhythm of your life in the neon-drenched metropolis. From waking in a modest lower-level apartment to inheriting your first ramen stand and returning home before the 2 AM curfew sirens, this chronological Day 1 walkthrough guides you through essential milestones without wasting early credits.

---

## Phase 1 (00:00–15:00): Awakening, Flat Familiarization & Meeting Ava

Your journey starts inside your personal starter flat in the lower levels of the city:

1. **Inspect Your Starter Apartment**:
   - Locate your bed—your primary anchor for sleeping, advancing time, and escaping curfew.
   - Note your kitchen brewing station for morning VegCoffee and basic storage cabinets.
2. **Step onto the Promenade**:
   - Follow the illuminated signs toward the waterfront docks. Keep an eye out for nearby HOVA taxi stands and public transit terminals.
3. **Encounter Ava at the Docks**:
   - Ava is your vital early contact in Nivalis. She grounds you in the city's geography, introduces you to canal waterways, and points you toward your commercial inheritance.

From the docks, follow the canal walkway into **Meridian Market**, one of Nivalis's oldest shopping sectors featuring 6 confirmed retail storefronts.

### Meeting Thaddeus Carmunis
Thaddeus is retiring from the grueling street-food trade. In the opening dialogue, he bequeaths you his legacy:
> *"You're taking over my old noodle bar. Ramen Noir."*

This gives you a functional commercial asset on Day 1 without an upfront mortgage.

### Meeting Banor in the Kitchen
Inside Ramen Noir, you meet **Banor**, an experienced line cook who remains on staff to assist with your first operational shift. Inspect the boiling broth vats, prep counter, and dry storage shelves.

## Phase 3 (30:00–45:00): Sourcing Ingredients & The Lunch Rush

Before flipping the "OPEN" sign on your counter, your kitchen pantry must be stocked:

1. **Procuring Starter Wholesale Stock**:
   - Head to neighboring merchant stalls in Meridian Market to buy bulk flour noodles, soup concentrate, and fresh scallions.
   - **Important Quest Tip (Update #1 Patch Fix)**: When completing the tutorial quest *"Fill Venue Inventory"*, purchase a small surplus of broth and noodles beyond the minimum checklist. Buying only exact minimal quantities can stall the completion trigger.
2. **Handling the Lunch Rush (11:30–14:00)**:
   - Position yourself behind the counter: take customer orders, manage the cooking stations alongside Banor, and assemble bowls promptly.
3. **Furniture & Plant Quest**:
   - When given the introductory quest to decorate Ramen Noir, purchase affordable houseplants and chairs from home decor retailers in **Eastern Residential**, **Hive Mall**, or **Central Canyon**.

---

## Phase 4 (45:00–60:00): Evening Downtime & The 2 AM Curfew Countdown

As the neon sun dips below the clouded horizon and evening sets in, the city shifts into nighttime mode:

- **01:00 AM Last Orders**: Close the customer counter and settle open tickets. Deposit your daily cash into the bank terminal to safeguard your revenue.
- **The 02:00 AM Curfew Rule**: Emergency sirens sound across all districts. Staying outdoors after 02:00 drains stamina rapidly, summons search drones, and triggers hospital collapse with a **1,200 Credits fine**.
- **Bed vs. Boat**: Either take a HOVA taxi back to your apartment bed, or anchor your motorboat in quiet canal water to sleep through the night.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the very first business you acquire in Nivalis Nights?
You take over Ramen Noir, a modest street noodle bar situated in Meridian Market, handed down to you when veteran restaurateur Thaddeus Carmunis retires. A kitchen worker named Banor is already present to assist you.

### Who is Ava, and where do you meet her early on?
Ava is your primary guide to the city's docks, waterways, and underground streets. You meet her along the waterfront docks during your opening morning.

### What happens if you are caught outside during the 2 AM curfew?
Failing to seek shelter in your apartment bed or rest aboard your boat before 02:00 AM exposes you to police drone sweeps, fatigue collapse, and a 1,200 Credits medical penalty fee.

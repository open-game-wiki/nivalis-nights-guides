## Read the match screen
For NPC minigames and chess, read the entry conditions, rules and stakes before accepting. Keep money for other city plans outside your match budget.

## Review the position
Before each move, check whether your king is safe and whether a piece is under attack. After a loss, return to the first move that lost material or allowed a direct threat.

## Check unusual moves
The October 1 patch fixed castling while in check. If a move seems wrong, keep the board position and build. See [tournament preparation](/guides/chess-tournament/) for a match checklist.

## The confirmed activity
The official feature list includes NPC minigames and a chess tournament. It does not publish a betting ratio, a complete opponent list or a guaranteed money-making loop.

## Read the match conditions
Before accepting a game, read any displayed rules and stakes. Keep funds for other plans instead of treating a win as certain. General chess practice can help you understand a position; it cannot guarantee how a game's opponent will play.

## Check unusual moves against updates
The October 1 patch fixed castling while a king was in check. If a move appears wrong, record the board state and build before deciding whether it is a special rule or a bug. See the [tournament preparation notes](https://nivalis-nights.online/guides/chess-tournament/).

## 📖 Key Strategy Highlights

### 1. Escaping the 50,000 Credit Debt (Days 1–7)
* **Never Sprint Empty-Handed**: Stamina depletion directly penalizes operating efficiency. Always carry basic meal rations when traveling between districts.
* **Fuel Reserve Protocol**: Always retain at least 15% hoverboat battery capacity before entering high-density skyway transit corridors to avoid costly corporate towing fees.
* **Hydroponic Scallion Loop**: Cultivating scallions and bok choy in your apartment greenhouse cuts Ramen Noir ingredient costs by ~38% compared to retail merchants.

### 2. Nightclub Management (Neon Haven & Spire Peak)
* **Demographic Matching**: Low-income patrons in *The Abyss* demand cheap synthetic spirits and high security, whereas executives in *Spire Peak* demand 300%+ margin craft cocktails and ambient synthwave DJs.
* **Staff Optimization**: Understaffed bars create severe queue bottlenecks, tanking customer hype. Maintain an optimal 2:1 bartender-to-bouncer ratio during peak midnight rushes.
* Full dynamic calculation formulas are available on the [Nightclub Simulator](https://nivalis-nights.online/nightclubs.html).
